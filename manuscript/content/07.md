# Gravy, Sauce & Casserole Base Mixes
* The secret to fast comfort food is a good sauce. With jars of gravy, white sauce and cheese sauce mix on the shelf, you can make smothered steaks, creamy casseroles and macaroni and cheese on a weeknight without opening a single packet.

## Brown Gravy Mix
> Toasting the flour first gives this gravy its rich color and roasted flavor.
@ Makes: about 1 3/4 cups (7 batches) | Prep: 15 minutes
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup Beef-Style Bouillon Powder or 1/3 cup beef bouillon granules
- 2 tablespoons onion powder
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon instant coffee granules (optional, for deeper color)
1. Place the flour in a dry skillet over medium heat. Stir constantly for 8 to 10 minutes, until it turns the color of light peanut butter and smells nutty.
2. Spread the flour on a plate and let it cool completely.
3. Whisk the cooled flour with the remaining ingredients and store in an airtight jar.
= How to Use
* For 1 cup of gravy: whisk 1/4 cup mix into 1 cup cold water in a saucepan. Add 1 tablespoon butter and cook, stirring, until thick and bubbly, about 3 minutes.
K: Pantry up to 3 months

## Country White Gravy Mix
> Creamy, peppery country gravy for biscuits, chicken fried steak and mashed potatoes.
@ Makes: about 3 cups (9 batches) | Prep: 5 minutes
- 2 cups nonfat dry milk powder
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 2 teaspoons coarsely ground black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
1. Whisk all the ingredients together.
2. Store in an airtight jar and label.
= How to Use
* For 1 cup of gravy: whisk 1/3 cup mix into 1 cup cold water. Add 1 tablespoon butter or pan drippings and cook, stirring, until thick and bubbly.
K: Pantry up to 6 months

## White Sauce Mix
> A shelf-stable white sauce (béchamel) base for creamed dishes, scalloped potatoes and casseroles.
@ Makes: about 3 cups (9 batches) | Prep: 5 minutes
- 2 cups nonfat dry milk powder
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
- 1/2 teaspoon white pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
1. Whisk all the ingredients together.
2. Store in an airtight jar and label.
= How to Use
* For 1 cup of medium white sauce: whisk 1/3 cup mix into 1 cup cold water. Add 2 tablespoons butter and cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until thick and smooth.
K: Pantry up to 6 months

## Cheese Sauce Mix
> Velvety cheese sauce in minutes for macaroni, vegetables, nachos and Amish haystacks.
@ Makes: about 2 1/2 cups (5 batches) | Prep: 5 minutes
- 1 cup cheddar cheese powder
- 1 cup nonfat dry milk powder
- 1/3 cup cornstarch
- 1 teaspoon dry mustard
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon sweet paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
1. Whisk all the ingredients together until the color is even.
2. Store in an airtight jar and label.
= How to Use
* For about 1 1/4 cups of sauce: whisk 1/2 cup mix into 1 cup cold water. Add 2 tablespoons butter and cook over medium heat, stirring, until thick and smooth.
K: Pantry up to 3 months; refrigerator up to 6 months
T: Cheddar cheese powder is sold online and in bulk stores. Look for one without added fillers for the best flavor.

## Herb Bread Stuffing Mix
> Turn stale bread into savory, herb-scented stuffing. Each batch replaces one 6-ounce box.
@ Makes: 3 batches | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 30 minutes
+ For the bread cubes
- 1 1/2 pounds white or wheat bread (about 18 slices), cut into 1/2-inch cubes (about 12 cups)
+ For the seasoning
- 1/4 cup Chicken-Style Bouillon Powder
- 2 tablespoons dried minced onion
- 2 tablespoons dried parsley flakes
- 2 teaspoons rubbed sage
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon celery seed
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
1. Heat the oven to 300°F. Spread the bread cubes on two baking sheets.
2. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, stirring halfway, until completely dry and crisp. Cool completely.
3. Divide the cubes into 3 bags of about 4 cups each.
4. Mix the seasoning ingredients and divide into 3 equal portions (about 3 tablespoons each). Add one portion to each bag of cubes.
= How to Use
* Bring 1 1/4 cups water and 1/4 cup butter to a boil. Remove from the heat, stir in 1 bag of stuffing mix, cover and let stand 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork.
K: Pantry up to 1 month; freezer up to 3 months

## Salisbury Steak with Brown Gravy
> Seasoned beef patties simmered in mushroom gravy. Serve over mashed potatoes.
@ Serves: 4 | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 25 minutes
U: Brown Gravy Mix, Homemade Onion Soup Mix
+ For the patties
- 1 1/2 pounds ground beef
- 1/3 cup dry bread crumbs
- 1 large egg
- 2 tablespoons Homemade Onion Soup Mix
+ For the gravy
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 8 ounces mushrooms, sliced
- 1/2 cup Brown Gravy Mix
- 2 cups cold water
1. Mix the beef, bread crumbs, egg and onion soup mix and shape into 4 oval patties about 3/4 inch thick.
2. Brown the patties in a large skillet over medium-high heat, about 4 minutes per side. Remove and drain the fat.
3. Melt the butter in the skillet and cook the mushrooms until golden.
4. Whisk the gravy mix into the cold water and pour into the skillet. Cook, stirring, until thickened.
5. Return the patties to the skillet, cover and simmer for 15 minutes, until cooked through (160°F inside).

## Hot Roast Beef Sandwiches
> A diner classic: tender roast beef on bread, smothered in hot brown gravy.
@ Serves: 4 | Prep: 5 minutes | Cook: 10 minutes
U: Brown Gravy Mix
- 1/2 cup Brown Gravy Mix
- 2 cups cold water
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 pound thinly sliced roast beef (leftover or deli)
- 8 slices white bread
- Mashed potatoes, to serve (optional)
1. Whisk the gravy mix into the cold water in a large skillet. Add the butter and cook, stirring, until thick.
2. Add the roast beef and simmer gently until heated through, 3 to 4 minutes.
3. Place 2 slices of bread on each plate, top with beef and spoon over plenty of gravy. Serve with mashed potatoes.

## Chicken Fried Steak with Country Gravy
> Crispy, golden steaks smothered in creamy pepper gravy: hearty farmhouse fare.
@ Serves: 4 | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 20 minutes
U: Country White Gravy Mix
+ For the steaks
- 4 beef cube steaks (about 4 ounces each)
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 large egg
- 1/2 cup milk
- Vegetable oil, for frying
+ For the gravy
- 2/3 cup Country White Gravy Mix
- 2 cups cold water
1. Mix the flour, salt, pepper and garlic powder in a shallow dish. Whisk the egg and milk in a second dish.
2. Dredge each steak in flour, dip in the egg mixture, then dredge in flour again, pressing to coat.
3. Heat 1/2 inch oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the steaks 3 to 4 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through. Drain on paper towels and keep warm.
4. Pour off all but 2 tablespoons of the oil. Whisk the gravy mix into the cold water, pour into the skillet and cook, stirring and scraping up the browned bits, until thick.
5. Spoon the gravy over the steaks.

## Stovetop Mac and Cheese
> Creamy, cheesy macaroni made in one pot in about 15 minutes.
@ Serves: 4 to 6 | Prep: 5 minutes | Cook: 15 minutes
U: Cheese Sauce Mix
- 8 ounces elbow macaroni
- 3/4 cup Cheese Sauce Mix
- 1 1/2 cups cold milk or water
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese (optional, for extra cheesiness)
1. Cook the macaroni in salted water according to the package directions. Drain.
2. In the same pot, whisk the cheese sauce mix into the cold milk. Add the butter and cook over medium heat, stirring, until thick and smooth.
3. Stir in the shredded cheese until melted, then add the macaroni and stir to coat.

## Cheesy Hamburger Skillet Pasta
> A homemade version of the boxed hamburger dinner, cooked in one skillet.
@ Serves: 4 to 6 | Prep: 5 minutes | Cook: 25 minutes
U: Cheese Sauce Mix
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 small onion, diced
- 2 cups water
- 1 cup milk
- 1/2 cup Cheese Sauce Mix
- 1 teaspoon Amish Seasoning Salt or 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 cups elbow macaroni (uncooked)
1. Brown the beef and onion in a large skillet. Drain the fat.
2. Whisk the water, milk, cheese sauce mix and seasoning salt in a bowl, then pour into the skillet.
3. Bring to a boil and stir in the macaroni.
4. Reduce the heat, cover and simmer for 12 to 14 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the pasta is tender and the sauce is creamy.

## Tuna Noodle Casserole
> An old-fashioned, budget-friendly casserole with a buttery cracker topping.
@ Serves: 6 | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 25 minutes
U: White Sauce Mix
- 8 ounces wide egg noodles
- 2/3 cup White Sauce Mix
- 2 cups cold water
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 cans (5 ounces each) tuna, drained
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 1 cup crushed buttery crackers mixed with 2 tablespoons melted butter
1. Heat the oven to 375°F and grease a 2-quart baking dish.
2. Cook the noodles until just tender and drain.
3. Whisk the white sauce mix into the cold water in a saucepan, add the butter and cook, stirring, until thick.
4. Stir in the noodles, tuna, peas and cheese. Spread in the baking dish.
5. Sprinkle with the buttered crackers and bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until bubbly.

## Amish Chicken and Stuffing Casserole
> Creamy chicken under a layer of golden herb stuffing. Sunday dinner comfort in one dish.
@ Serves: 6 | Prep: 20 minutes | Cook: 35 minutes
U: Herb Bread Stuffing Mix, Cream Soup Base Mix (Condensed Soup Substitute)
- 2/3 cup Cream Soup Base Mix
- 2 1/2 cups cold chicken broth or water
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 3 cups diced cooked chicken
- 1 cup frozen green beans or mixed vegetables
- 1 bag Herb Bread Stuffing Mix
- 1 1/4 cups water
- 1/4 cup butter
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9 x 13-inch baking dish.
2. Whisk the soup base into the cold broth in a saucepan and cook, stirring, until thick. Remove from the heat and stir in the sour cream.
3. Stir in the chicken and vegetables and spread in the baking dish.
4. Bring the water and butter to a boil, remove from the heat and stir in the stuffing mix. Spoon over the chicken.
5. Bake uncovered for 30 to 35 minutes, until bubbly and golden on top.

## Scalloped Potatoes
> Thin potato slices baked in a creamy sauce until tender and golden.
@ Serves: 8 | Prep: 20 minutes | Cook: 1 hour 30 minutes
U: White Sauce Mix
- 2/3 cup White Sauce Mix
- 2 cups cold water
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 pounds russet potatoes, peeled and thinly sliced
- 1 small onion, thinly sliced
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese (optional)
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a 2-quart baking dish.
2. Whisk the white sauce mix into the cold water in a saucepan, add the butter and cook, stirring, until thick.
3. Layer one-third of the potatoes and onion in the dish and top with one-third of the sauce. Repeat twice.
4. Cover with foil and bake for 1 hour.
5. Uncover, sprinkle with cheese if using, and bake 20 to 30 minutes more, until the potatoes are tender and the top is golden.

## Creamed Chipped Beef on Toast
> A thrifty, old-fashioned breakfast or supper of salty dried beef in creamy sauce.
@ Serves: 4 | Prep: 5 minutes | Cook: 10 minutes
U: White Sauce Mix
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 4 ounces dried beef, chopped
- 1/2 cup White Sauce Mix
- 1 1/2 cups cold water or milk
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 8 slices toast
1. Melt the butter in a skillet and cook the dried beef for 2 minutes.
2. Whisk the white sauce mix into the cold water and pour into the skillet.
3. Cook, stirring, until thick and bubbly. Season with pepper. The beef is salty, so taste before adding salt.
4. Spoon over hot toast.

## Amish Haystack Supper
> A fun, build-your-own Amish favorite for church suppers and large gatherings. Everyone piles up their own haystack and tops it with warm cheese sauce.
@ Serves: 8 | Prep: 30 minutes | Cook: 20 minutes
U: Cheese Sauce Mix, Homemade Taco Seasoning
+ For the beef
- 2 pounds ground beef
- 1/4 cup Homemade Taco Seasoning
- 1 cup water
+ For the cheese sauce
- 1 cup Cheese Sauce Mix
- 2 cups cold milk
- 1/4 cup butter
+ For the layers
- 1 sleeve saltine crackers, crushed
- 4 cups cooked rice
- 1 head lettuce, shredded
- 3 tomatoes, diced
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
- Corn chips, salsa and sour cream
1. Brown the beef in a large skillet and drain. Stir in the taco seasoning and water and simmer until thickened, about 5 minutes.
2. Whisk the cheese sauce mix into the cold milk in a saucepan. Add the butter and cook, stirring, until smooth and thick. Keep warm.
3. Set out all the layers in bowls, in this order: crackers, rice, beef, lettuce, tomatoes, onion, shredded cheese and corn chips.
4. Let everyone build their own haystack and finish with warm cheese sauce, salsa and sour cream.
