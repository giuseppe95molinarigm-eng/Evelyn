# Yeast Bread & Roll Mixes
* Homemade bread is at the heart of every Amish table. These mixes hold everything except the yeast and the liquids, pre-measured and ready. The yeast is added on baking day so the mix keeps longer and the bread always rises well.

## Amish White Bread Mix
> Soft, slightly sweet sandwich bread, the loaf found in nearly every Amish kitchen. Each bag makes two loaves.
@ Makes: 2 bags (2 loaves each) | Prep: 10 minutes
+ For each bag
- 6 cups bread flour
- 2/3 cup granulated sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
1. For each bag, whisk the ingredients together and pour into a large zip-top bag or jar. Repeat to make a second bag.
2. Label each bag with the baking directions below.
= How to Use
* For 2 loaves you will also need 2 cups warm water (110°F), 4 1/2 teaspoons (2 packets) instant yeast and 1/4 cup vegetable oil. See Amish White Bread for the full method.
K: Pantry up to 6 months

## Honey Whole Wheat Bread Mix
> A wholesome, lightly sweet wheat bread. A little vital wheat gluten keeps it soft and high.
@ Makes: 2 bags (2 loaves each) | Prep: 10 minutes
+ For each bag
- 3 cups whole wheat flour
- 3 cups bread flour
- 1/3 cup nonfat dry milk powder
- 2 tablespoons vital wheat gluten (optional, for a softer loaf)
- 2 teaspoons salt
1. For each bag, whisk the ingredients together and pour into a large zip-top bag. Repeat to make a second bag.
= How to Use
* For 2 loaves you will also need 2 1/4 cups warm water, 1/3 cup honey, 1/4 cup vegetable oil and 4 1/2 teaspoons instant yeast. See Honey Oat Wheat Bread for the full method.
K: Pantry up to 1 month; refrigerator or freezer up to 6 months

## Soft Dinner Roll Mix
> Pillowy, buttery rolls for holidays and Sunday dinners. One bag makes 24 rolls or 12 hamburger buns.
@ Makes: 2 bags | Prep: 10 minutes
+ For each bag
- 6 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup nonfat dry milk powder
- 2 teaspoons salt
1. For each bag, whisk the ingredients together and pour into a large zip-top bag. Repeat to make a second bag.
= How to Use
* For 24 rolls you will also need 1 3/4 cups warm water, 2 eggs, 1/2 cup melted butter and 4 1/2 teaspoons instant yeast. See Soft Amish Dinner Rolls for the full method.
K: Pantry up to 3 months

## Pizza Dough Mix
> Herb-flecked dough for homemade pizza, stromboli, garlic knots and soft pretzels.
@ Makes: 3 bags (two 12-inch crusts each) | Prep: 10 minutes
+ For each bag
- 4 1/2 cups bread flour or all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1 teaspoon granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
1. For each bag, whisk the ingredients together and pour into a zip-top bag. Repeat to make 3 bags.
= How to Use
* Stir 1 bag of mix with 2 1/4 teaspoons (1 packet) instant yeast, 1 3/4 cups warm water and 2 tablespoons olive oil. Knead for 5 minutes and let rise until doubled, about 1 hour.
K: Pantry up to 6 months

## Cinnamon Roll Mix
> A complete kit for a pan of soft, gooey cinnamon rolls: dough mix plus its own cinnamon filling.
@ Makes: 2 kits (12 rolls each) | Prep: 15 minutes
+ For each dough bag
- 4 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/3 cup nonfat dry milk powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
+ For each filling bag
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 2 1/2 tablespoons ground cinnamon
1. For each kit, whisk the dough ingredients together and pour into a large zip-top bag.
2. Mix the brown sugar and cinnamon and seal in a smaller bag. Tuck it inside or tape it to the dough bag.
3. Repeat to make a second kit.
= How to Use
* You will also need 2 1/4 teaspoons instant yeast, 1 cup warm water, 2 eggs, 1/3 cup melted butter and 1/3 cup softened butter for the filling. See Amish Cinnamon Rolls.
K: Pantry up to 3 months

## Amish White Bread
> Soft, tender and lightly sweet. It makes wonderful toast and sandwiches.
@ Makes: 2 loaves | Prep: 20 minutes, plus 1 1/2 hours rising | Cook: 35 minutes
U: Amish White Bread Mix
- 2 cups warm water (110°F)
- 4 1/2 teaspoons instant yeast
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil
- 1 bag Amish White Bread Mix
- 1 tablespoon melted butter, for brushing
1. Combine the warm water and yeast in a large bowl. Add the oil and about half of the bread mix and stir well.
2. Gradually stir in the remaining mix until a soft dough forms.
3. Knead on a floured surface for 6 to 8 minutes, until smooth and elastic.
4. Place in a greased bowl, cover and let rise in a warm place until doubled, about 1 hour.
5. Punch down, divide in half and shape into 2 loaves. Place in greased 9 x 5-inch loaf pans.
6. Cover and let rise until the dough crowns about 1 inch above the pans, 30 to 45 minutes.
7. Bake at 350°F for 30 to 35 minutes, until golden and the loaves sound hollow when tapped (190°F inside). Brush the tops with butter and cool on a rack.

## Honey Oat Wheat Bread
> Hearty wheat bread with honey and oats on top. Wonderful with butter and apple butter.
@ Makes: 2 loaves | Prep: 20 minutes, plus 1 1/2 hours rising | Cook: 40 minutes
U: Honey Whole Wheat Bread Mix
- 2 1/2 cups warm water (110°F)
- 1/3 cup honey
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil
- 4 1/2 teaspoons instant yeast
- 1 bag Honey Whole Wheat Bread Mix
- 1 cup old-fashioned rolled oats, plus 2 tablespoons for topping
- 1 egg white, beaten
1. Stir together the water, honey, oil and yeast in a large bowl.
2. Add the oats and half the bread mix and beat well. Stir in the rest of the mix to make a soft dough.
3. Knead for 8 minutes, until smooth. Cover and let rise until doubled, about 1 hour.
4. Shape into 2 loaves and place in greased 9 x 5-inch loaf pans. Brush with egg white and sprinkle with oats.
5. Let rise until nearly doubled, about 40 minutes.
6. Bake at 350°F for 35 to 40 minutes, until browned and hollow-sounding (190°F inside).

## Soft Amish Dinner Rolls
> Light, golden, buttery rolls that pull apart into soft, steamy pieces.
@ Makes: 24 rolls | Prep: 25 minutes, plus 1 1/2 hours rising | Cook: 18 minutes
U: Soft Dinner Roll Mix
- 1 3/4 cups warm water (110°F)
- 4 1/2 teaspoons instant yeast
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup melted butter, plus more for brushing
- 1 bag Soft Dinner Roll Mix
1. Combine the water, yeast, eggs and butter in a large bowl. Stir in the roll mix to make a soft dough.
2. Knead for 5 to 7 minutes, until smooth. The dough should be slightly tacky.
3. Cover and let rise until doubled, about 1 hour.
4. Divide into 24 pieces and roll each into a smooth ball. Arrange in two greased 9 x 13-inch pans, 12 rolls in each.
5. Cover and let rise until puffy, about 30 minutes.
6. Bake at 375°F for 15 to 18 minutes, until golden. Brush with melted butter while hot.

## Garlic Butter Knots
> Soft knots of pizza dough brushed with garlic butter and Parmesan. Serve them with soup, pasta or pizza night.
@ Makes: 16 knots | Prep: 25 minutes, plus 1 hour rising | Cook: 15 minutes
U: Pizza Dough Mix
- 1 batch Pizza Dough Mix dough, risen
+ For the garlic butter
- 1/4 cup butter, melted
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley or 1 teaspoon dried
- 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese
1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Divide the dough into 16 pieces. Roll each into an 8-inch rope and tie in a loose knot.
3. Place on the baking sheet and bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until golden.
4. Stir together the butter, garlic and parsley. Brush over the hot knots and sprinkle with Parmesan.

## Homemade Hamburger Buns
> Soft, sturdy buns that hold up to juicy burgers and pulled pork.
@ Makes: 12 buns | Prep: 25 minutes, plus 1 1/2 hours rising | Cook: 18 minutes
U: Soft Dinner Roll Mix
- 1 batch Soft Dinner Roll Mix dough (prepared through the first rise)
- 1 egg, beaten with 1 tablespoon water
- Sesame seeds (optional)
1. Divide the risen dough into 12 pieces and roll into balls.
2. Place on parchment-lined baking sheets and flatten each ball into a 3 1/2-inch disk.
3. Cover and let rise until puffy, about 30 minutes.
4. Brush with egg wash and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
5. Bake at 375°F for 15 to 18 minutes, until golden. Cool on a rack before slicing.

## Friday Night Pizza
> Two homemade pizzas, ready for your family’s favorite toppings.
@ Makes: two 12-inch pizzas | Prep: 20 minutes, plus 1 hour rising | Cook: 15 minutes
U: Pizza Dough Mix
- 1 bag Pizza Dough Mix
- 2 1/4 teaspoons instant yeast
- 1 3/4 cups warm water (110°F)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 cup pizza sauce
- 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- Toppings: pepperoni, cooked sausage, peppers, onions, mushrooms
1. Stir the pizza dough mix and yeast together. Add the water and oil and mix into a dough.
2. Knead for 5 minutes, until smooth. Cover and let rise until doubled, about 1 hour.
3. Heat the oven to 450°F. Divide the dough in half and press each piece into a greased 12-inch pizza pan.
4. Spread with sauce, sprinkle with cheese and add your toppings.
5. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.

## Ham and Cheese Stromboli
> A rolled-up pizza loaf filled with ham and melted cheese. Slice it for an easy supper or a party platter.
@ Serves: 6 | Prep: 20 minutes | Cook: 25 minutes
U: Pizza Dough Mix
- Half of 1 batch Pizza Dough Mix dough, risen
- 8 ounces thinly sliced deli ham
- 6 slices provolone cheese
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1/2 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- Warm pizza sauce, for dipping
1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Roll the dough into a 10 x 14-inch rectangle.
3. Layer the ham, provolone and mozzarella over the dough, leaving a 1-inch border.
4. Starting from a long side, roll up tightly. Pinch the seam and ends to seal and place seam side down on the baking sheet.
5. Brush with egg, sprinkle with Italian seasoning and cut 4 small slits in the top.
6. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until deep golden. Rest 5 minutes, then slice and serve with sauce.

## Amish Cinnamon Rolls
> Big, soft rolls swirled with brown sugar and cinnamon and finished with cream cheese icing.
@ Makes: 12 rolls | Prep: 30 minutes, plus 1 1/2 hours rising | Cook: 30 minutes
U: Cinnamon Roll Mix
+ For the dough
- 1 Cinnamon Roll Mix kit (dough bag and filling bag)
- 2 1/4 teaspoons instant yeast
- 1 cup warm water (110°F)
- 2 large eggs
- 1/3 cup melted butter
+ For the filling
- 1/3 cup butter, very soft
+ For the icing
- 4 ounces cream cheese, softened
- 2 tablespoons butter, softened
- 1 1/2 cups powdered sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 to 2 tablespoons milk
1. Combine the dough mix and yeast. Add the water, eggs and melted butter and mix into a soft dough.
2. Knead for 5 minutes, until smooth. Cover and let rise until doubled, about 1 hour.
3. Roll the dough into a 16 x 12-inch rectangle. Spread with the soft butter and sprinkle evenly with the cinnamon filling.
4. Roll up from a long side and cut into 12 slices. Arrange in a greased 9 x 13-inch pan.
5. Cover and let rise until puffy, about 30 minutes.
6. Bake at 350°F for 25 to 30 minutes, until golden.
7. Beat the icing ingredients until smooth and spread over the warm rolls.
T: Use unflavored dental floss to cut clean slices without squashing the rolls.

## Soft Pretzels
> Chewy, golden pretzels with a salty crust, just like the ones sold at Amish market stands.
@ Makes: 8 large pretzels | Prep: 30 minutes, plus 1 hour rising | Cook: 14 minutes
U: Pizza Dough Mix
- 1 batch Pizza Dough Mix dough, risen
- 10 cups water
- 2/3 cup baking soda
- 1 egg, beaten with 1 tablespoon water
- Coarse salt, for sprinkling
1. Heat the oven to 425°F and line two baking sheets with greased parchment paper.
2. Bring the water and baking soda to a boil in a large, wide pot.
3. Divide the dough into 8 pieces. Roll each into a 24-inch rope, form a U shape, twist the ends together twice and press them onto the bottom of the U.
4. Boil each pretzel for 30 seconds, then lift it out with a slotted spatula and place it on the baking sheet.
5. Brush with egg wash and sprinkle with coarse salt.
6. Bake for 12 to 14 minutes, until deep golden brown.

## Pull-Apart Monkey Bread
> Sticky, gooey balls of sweet dough baked in a Bundt pan. Pull off a piece and enjoy.
@ Serves: 10 to 12 | Prep: 30 minutes, plus 1 1/2 hours rising | Cook: 35 minutes
U: Cinnamon Roll Mix
- 1 Cinnamon Roll Mix kit, dough prepared through the first rise
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 3/4 cup butter, melted
1. Grease a 10-inch Bundt pan well.
2. In a shallow bowl, mix the cinnamon filling from the kit with the granulated sugar.
3. Pinch the risen dough into about 48 small balls. Dip each in melted butter, roll in the cinnamon sugar and layer in the pan.
4. Stir any leftover cinnamon sugar into the remaining butter and pour over the top.
5. Cover and let rise for 30 minutes.
6. Bake at 350°F for 30 to 35 minutes, until deep golden. Cool for 5 minutes, then invert onto a plate.
