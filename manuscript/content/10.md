# Gifts in a Jar
* A beautifully layered jar of homemade mix is one of the most welcome gifts you can give: thoughtful, useful and made by hand. Every recipe in this chapter fits in a 1-quart (32-ounce) wide-mouth jar unless noted, and comes with ready-to-copy gift tag directions. Pack each layer firmly with the back of a spoon, wipe the inside of the jar with a dry paper towel between layers to keep the lines clean, then top with a circle of fabric and tie on the tag with twine.

## Cowboy Cookies in a Jar
> A hearty cookie packed with oats, chocolate chips and pecans.
@ Makes: 1 quart jar (about 3 dozen cookies)
+ In the jar, layered in this order
- 1 1/3 cups all-purpose flour mixed with 1 teaspoon baking powder, 1 teaspoon baking soda and 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/2 cup chopped pecans
- 1 1/3 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
- 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips
1. Pour the flour mixture into the jar and pack it down.
2. Add each remaining layer in order, packing firmly after each one. Seal tightly.
= Gift Tag
* Cowboy Cookies: Empty the jar into a large bowl and mix well. Add 1/2 cup softened butter, 1 egg and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract and mix until a dough forms. Drop by tablespoons onto a greased baking sheet. Bake at 350°F for 11 to 13 minutes. Makes about 3 dozen.

## Chocolate Chip Cookies in a Jar
> The classic cookie everyone loves, ready to bake.
@ Makes: 1 quart jar (about 2 1/2 dozen cookies)
+ In the jar, layered in this order
- 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour mixed with 1 teaspoon baking soda and 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1 1/4 cups semisweet chocolate chips
1. Pour the flour mixture into the jar and pack it down.
2. Add the brown sugar, granulated sugar and chocolate chips, packing after each layer. Seal tightly.
= Gift Tag
* Chocolate Chip Cookies: Empty the jar into a large bowl and mix well. Add 3/4 cup softened butter, 1 egg and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract and mix until a dough forms. Drop by rounded tablespoons onto an ungreased baking sheet. Bake at 375°F for 9 to 11 minutes. Makes about 2 1/2 dozen.

## Brownies in a Jar
> Fudgy, nutty brownies with a striking layered look in the jar.
@ Makes: 1 quart jar (one 9 x 13-inch pan)
+ In the jar, layered in this order
- 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour mixed with 1 teaspoon baking powder and 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 1/4 cups granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup chopped pecans or walnuts
1. Pour the flour mixture into the jar and pack it down.
2. Spoon in the cocoa carefully, wiping the inside of the jar clean, then add the sugar and nuts, packing each layer. Seal tightly.
= Gift Tag
* Brownies: Empty the jar into a large bowl and mix well. Add 3/4 cup melted butter, 4 eggs and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract and stir until smooth. Spread in a greased 9 x 13-inch pan. Bake at 350°F for 25 to 30 minutes. Cool before cutting.

## Gingerbread Cookies in a Jar
> Spicy, molasses-rich gingerbread for cutting into holiday shapes.
@ Makes: 1 quart jar (about 3 dozen cookies)
+ In the jar, layered in this order
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour mixed with 1 tablespoon ground ginger, 1 teaspoon baking soda and 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2/3 cup packed brown sugar
- 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour mixed with 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon and 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves
1. Pour the first flour mixture into the jar and pack it down.
2. Add the brown sugar and pack firmly, then add the second flour mixture and pack. Seal tightly.
= Gift Tag
* Gingerbread Cookies: Empty the jar into a large bowl and mix well. Add 1/2 cup softened butter, 1/2 cup molasses and 1 egg and mix into a stiff dough. Chill for 1 hour. Roll out 1/4 inch thick on a floured surface and cut into shapes. Bake at 350°F for 8 to 10 minutes. Makes about 3 dozen.

## Snickerdoodles in a Jar
> Soft, tangy cookies rolled in cinnamon sugar, with the coating packed right in the jar.
@ Makes: 1 quart jar (about 4 dozen cookies)
+ In the jar, layered in this order
- 2 3/4 cups all-purpose flour mixed with 2 teaspoons cream of tartar, 1 teaspoon baking soda and 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
- 1 small bag containing 1/4 cup granulated sugar mixed with 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
1. Pour the flour mixture into the jar and pack down very firmly.
2. Add the sugar and pack firmly. Place the cinnamon-sugar bag on top. Seal tightly.
= Gift Tag
* Snickerdoodles: Remove the cinnamon-sugar bag. Empty the jar into a large bowl and mix well. Add 1 cup softened butter, 2 eggs and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract and mix into a dough. Roll into 1-inch balls and roll in the cinnamon sugar. Bake at 375°F for 8 to 10 minutes. Makes about 4 dozen.

## Cranberry White Chip Oatmeal Cookies in a Jar
> Chewy oatmeal cookies with tart cranberries and creamy white chocolate. A festive favorite.
@ Makes: 1 quart jar (about 2 dozen cookies)
+ In the jar, layered in this order
- 1 cup all-purpose flour mixed with 1/2 teaspoon baking soda, 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon and 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 1 1/4 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
- 1/2 cup dried cranberries
- 1/2 cup white chocolate chips
1. Pour the flour mixture into the jar and pack it down.
2. Add each remaining layer in order, packing after each one. Seal tightly.
= Gift Tag
* Cranberry White Chip Oatmeal Cookies: Empty the jar into a large bowl and mix well. Add 1/2 cup softened butter, 1 egg and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract and mix until combined. Drop by tablespoons onto a greased baking sheet. Bake at 350°F for 10 to 12 minutes. Makes about 2 dozen.

## Buttermilk Pancakes in a Jar
> A breakfast gift for new neighbors or newlyweds. Pair it with a small bottle of maple syrup.
@ Makes: 1 quart jar (about 2 batches of pancakes)
U: Old-Fashioned Buttermilk Pancake Mix
+ In the jar
- 3 1/2 cups Old-Fashioned Buttermilk Pancake Mix
1. Spoon the pancake mix into the jar, tapping the jar gently to settle it. Seal tightly.
2. For a layered look, make a fresh batch of the mix directly in the jar: add the flour first, then the buttermilk powder, the sugar, and finally the leavening and salt, scaling the master recipe down by half.
= Gift Tag
* Buttermilk Pancakes: Shake the jar well. Whisk 1 1/2 cups mix with 1 egg, 1 1/4 cups water and 2 tablespoons vegetable oil just until combined. Cook 1/4 cup portions on a hot, greased griddle until golden on both sides. Makes 10 to 12 pancakes per batch.

## Honey Cornbread in a Jar
> Sweet, tender cornbread mix, lovely with a jar of local honey tied to the lid.
@ Makes: 1 quart jar (one 8-inch pan)
+ In the jar, layered in this order
- 1 1/4 cups yellow cornmeal
- 1/3 cup nonfat dry milk powder
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour mixed with 1 tablespoon baking powder and 1/2 teaspoon salt
1. Pour the cornmeal into the jar and pack it down.
2. Add the milk powder, sugar and flour mixture, packing after each layer. Seal tightly.
= Gift Tag
* Honey Cornbread: Empty the jar into a bowl and mix well. Add 1 egg, 1 cup water, 1/3 cup melted butter and 1/4 cup honey and stir just until combined. Pour into a greased 8-inch pan. Bake at 400°F for 20 to 25 minutes.

## Friendship Soup in a Jar
> A colorful layered soup mix of beans, grains and pasta that becomes a big pot of hearty beef soup.
@ Makes: 1 quart jar (10 to 12 servings)
+ In the jar, layered in this order
- 1/2 cup green split peas
- 1/3 cup Beef-Style Bouillon Powder or beef bouillon granules
- 1/4 cup pearl barley
- 1/2 cup brown lentils
- 1/4 cup dried minced onion
- 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning
- 1/2 cup long-grain white rice
- 1 small bag containing 1/2 cup alphabet or other small pasta
1. Layer the ingredients in the order listed, tapping the jar to level each layer.
2. Place the bag of pasta on top. Seal tightly.
= Gift Tag
* Friendship Soup: Remove the pasta bag. In a large pot, brown 1 pound ground beef and drain. Add 12 cups water, one 28-ounce can diced tomatoes and the remaining jar contents. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer for 45 minutes. Add the pasta and simmer 15 minutes more, until tender. Serves 10 to 12.

## Chicken Noodle Soup in a Jar
> Everything you need for a comforting pot of chicken noodle soup. Just add chicken.
@ Makes: 1 quart jar (6 servings)
+ In the jar, layered in this order
- 1/4 cup Chicken-Style Bouillon Powder or chicken bouillon granules
- 2 tablespoons dried minced onion
- 1/4 cup dried carrot flakes
- 1 tablespoon dried celery flakes
- 1 tablespoon dried parsley flakes
- 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 bay leaf
- 2 1/2 cups wide egg noodles
1. Mix the bouillon powder, minced onion, carrot, celery, parsley, thyme and pepper and pour into the jar. Add the bay leaf.
2. Fill the jar with the noodles. Seal tightly.
= Gift Tag
* Chicken Noodle Soup: Bring 8 cups water to a boil. Add the jar contents and 2 cups diced cooked chicken. Simmer for 10 to 12 minutes, until the noodles are tender. Remove the bay leaf before serving. Serves 6.

## Nine-Bean Soup in a Jar
> A rainbow of dried beans in a jar, a classic homestead gift that becomes a slow-simmered soup.
@ Makes: 1 pint jar (8 servings)
+ In the jar, layered in this order
- 1/4 cup each of 9 dried beans and legumes, such as navy beans, pinto beans, black beans, kidney beans, great northern beans, black-eyed peas, green split peas, yellow split peas and lentils
+ For the seasoning packet
- 2 tablespoons Chicken-Style Bouillon Powder
- 1 tablespoon dried minced onion
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 bay leaf
1. Layer the beans in the jar in contrasting colors.
2. Mix the seasoning ingredients in a small bag and tie it to the lid. Seal tightly.
= Gift Tag
* Nine-Bean Soup: Rinse the beans, cover with water and soak overnight. Drain. Place in a large pot with 8 cups water and a ham hock or 1 cup diced ham, if you like. Boil hard for 10 minutes, then reduce the heat, cover and simmer for 1 1/2 to 2 hours, until tender. Add one 14.5-ounce can diced tomatoes and the seasoning packet and simmer 30 minutes more. Remove the bay leaf. Serves 8.
T: Kidney beans must be boiled hard for at least 10 minutes to be safe to eat. Do not cook this soup from dry in a slow cooker. Boil the beans on the stove first.

## Layered Hot Cocoa in a Jar
> A cheerful jar of cocoa topped with marshmallows. Pair it with a pair of mugs for a perfect winter gift.
@ Makes: 1 quart jar (about 8 servings)
+ In the jar, layered in this order
- 1 cup nonfat dry milk powder
- 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup semisweet chocolate chips
- 1 cup mini marshmallows
1. Add the milk powder and pack it down. Spoon in the cocoa carefully and wipe the inside of the jar.
2. Add the sugar, chocolate chips and marshmallows. Seal tightly.
= Gift Tag
* Hot Cocoa: Pour the jar contents into a bowl and mix well, setting aside the marshmallows if you prefer. For each mug, stir 1/3 cup mix into 1 cup hot milk or water. Top with marshmallows. Makes about 8 servings.

## Cappuccino Mix in a Jar
> A creamy, cinnamon-kissed instant cappuccino, ideal for coffee lovers and teachers.
@ Makes: 1 quart jar (about 20 servings)
+ In the jar
- 1 cup nonfat dry milk powder
- 1 cup powdered coffee creamer
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 2/3 cup instant coffee granules
- 2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
1. Blend all the ingredients in a blender until fine and powdery.
2. Spoon into the jar and seal tightly.
= Gift Tag
* Cappuccino: Stir 3 tablespoons mix into 3/4 cup hot water or hot milk. Top with whipped cream and a sprinkle of cinnamon. Contains caffeine.

## Mulling Spices in a Jar
> Fill the house with the smell of the holidays. Simmer with apple cider or grape juice.
@ Makes: 1 half-pint jar (about 6 uses)
+ In the jar
- 1/2 cup cinnamon sticks, broken into pieces
- 1/4 cup dried orange peel
- 2 tablespoons whole cloves
- 2 tablespoons whole allspice berries
- 6 whole star anise
1. Mix all the ingredients in a bowl and spoon into the jar. Seal tightly.
2. Include a few small muslin bags or squares of cheesecloth with the gift.
= Gift Tag
* Mulling Spices: Tie 2 tablespoons spices in a cheesecloth bag. Simmer with 1/2 gallon apple cider or grape juice for 15 to 30 minutes. Remove the bag and serve hot. Add orange slices if you like.

## Pumpkin Spice Bread in a Jar
> A warmly spiced quick bread mix for fall and Thanksgiving gifting.
@ Makes: 1 quart jar (one 9 x 5-inch loaf)
+ In the jar, layered in this order
- 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour mixed with 1 teaspoon baking soda, 1/2 teaspoon baking powder, 1/2 teaspoon salt and 2 teaspoons pumpkin pie spice
- 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup chopped pecans
1. Pour the flour mixture into the jar and pack it down.
2. Add the brown sugar, granulated sugar and pecans, packing after each layer. Seal tightly.
= Gift Tag
* Pumpkin Spice Bread: Empty the jar into a large bowl and mix well. Add 1 cup canned pumpkin, 2 eggs, 1/2 cup vegetable oil and 1/3 cup water and stir until combined. Pour into a greased 9 x 5-inch loaf pan. Bake at 350°F for 60 to 65 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
