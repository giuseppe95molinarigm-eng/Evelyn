# Hot Drink, Pudding & Sweet Treat Mixes
* Cozy drinks and simple, old-fashioned desserts for cold evenings, church suppers and unexpected guests. Keep these jars near the kettle and you will always have something warm and sweet to offer.

## Creamy Hot Cocoa Mix
> Rich, creamy and chocolatey, ready in seconds with hot water or milk.
@ Makes: about 4 cups (12 servings) | Prep: 5 minutes
- 2 cups nonfat dry milk powder
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 3/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/2 cup powdered coffee creamer (optional, for extra creaminess)
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup mini chocolate chips (optional)
1. Sift or whisk the milk powder, powdered sugar, cocoa, creamer and salt together until there are no lumps. Stir in the chocolate chips.
2. Store in an airtight jar and label.
= How to Use
* For 1 mug: stir 1/3 cup mix into 1 cup hot water or hot milk.
K: Pantry up to 6 months

## Spiced Tea Mix
> A sweet, tangy, spiced orange tea, sometimes called friendship tea. It makes a lovely winter gift.
@ Makes: about 3 cups | Prep: 5 minutes
- 1 cup orange-flavored breakfast drink powder
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup unsweetened instant tea powder
- 1/2 cup sweetened lemonade drink powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cloves
1. Stir all the ingredients together until evenly blended.
2. Store in an airtight jar and label.
= How to Use
* For 1 mug: stir 2 to 3 teaspoons mix into 1 cup boiling water.
K: Pantry up to 6 months

## Vanilla Pudding Mix
> Smooth, creamy homemade vanilla pudding without a boxed mix.
@ Makes: about 4 cups (4 batches) | Prep: 5 minutes
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 1 1/3 cups nonfat dry milk powder
- 3/4 cup cornstarch
- 1 teaspoon salt
1. Whisk all the ingredients together until evenly blended.
2. Store in an airtight jar and label.
= How to Use
* For 4 servings: whisk 1 cup mix with 2 cups cold milk in a saucepan. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until thick and bubbling. Remove from the heat and stir in 1 tablespoon butter and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract.
K: Pantry up to 6 months

## Chocolate Pudding Mix
> Deep, dark chocolate pudding for spooning, layering and filling pies.
@ Makes: about 4 1/2 cups (4 batches) | Prep: 5 minutes
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 1 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 cup nonfat dry milk powder
- 3/4 cup cornstarch
- 1 teaspoon salt
1. Sift or whisk all the ingredients together until there are no cocoa lumps.
2. Store in an airtight jar and label.
= How to Use
* For 4 servings: whisk 1 cup plus 2 tablespoons mix with 2 1/4 cups cold milk in a saucepan. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until thick and bubbling. Stir in 1 tablespoon butter and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract.
K: Pantry up to 6 months

## Fruit Crisp Topping Mix
> A buttery, crunchy oat topping for any fruit crisp, from apples in fall to berries in summer.
@ Makes: about 7 cups | Prep: 5 minutes
- 3 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups packed brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg
1. Mix all the ingredients together, breaking up the brown sugar.
2. Store in an airtight container and label.
= How to Use
* For an 8-inch square crisp: rub 1/2 cup cold butter into 1 3/4 cups mix. For a 9 x 13-inch crisp: rub 3/4 cup cold butter into 3 cups mix.
K: Pantry up to 3 months

## Rich Hot Chocolate
> A velvety mug of cocoa made with milk and topped with whipped cream.
@ Serves: 4 | Prep: 2 minutes | Cook: 5 minutes
U: Creamy Hot Cocoa Mix
- 4 cups whole milk
- 1 1/3 cups Creamy Hot Cocoa Mix
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Whipped cream or marshmallows, to serve
1. Heat the milk in a saucepan over medium heat until steaming. Do not boil.
2. Whisk in the cocoa mix until smooth and frothy, then stir in the vanilla.
3. Pour into mugs and top with whipped cream or marshmallows.

## Peppermint Mocha
> A coffee-shop holiday favorite made at home for a fraction of the price.
@ Serves: 1 | Prep: 5 minutes
U: Creamy Hot Cocoa Mix
- 1/3 cup Creamy Hot Cocoa Mix
- 1/2 cup hot strong coffee
- 1/2 cup hot milk
- 1/4 teaspoon peppermint extract
- Whipped cream and crushed peppermint candy, to serve
1. Stir the cocoa mix into the hot coffee until dissolved.
2. Add the hot milk and peppermint extract and stir.
3. Top with whipped cream and crushed peppermint.

## Hot Spiced Tea
> A big batch of warming spiced tea for holiday gatherings. It keeps warm in a slow cooker.
@ Serves: 8 | Prep: 5 minutes | Cook: 10 minutes
U: Spiced Tea Mix
- 8 cups water
- 1/2 cup Spiced Tea Mix, or to taste
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 1 orange, thinly sliced
1. Bring the water to a boil in a large pot.
2. Stir in the tea mix until dissolved. Add the cinnamon sticks and orange slices.
3. Simmer gently for 5 minutes. Serve hot, or keep warm in a slow cooker on low.

## Homemade Vanilla Pudding
> Silky, comforting pudding to eat warm or chilled.
@ Serves: 4 | Prep: 2 minutes | Cook: 8 minutes
U: Vanilla Pudding Mix
- 1 cup Vanilla Pudding Mix
- 2 cups cold milk
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
1. Whisk the pudding mix and cold milk in a saucepan until smooth.
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the pudding thickens and bubbles. Cook and stir 1 minute more.
3. Remove from the heat and stir in the butter and vanilla.
4. Pour into dishes. Press plastic wrap onto the surface to prevent a skin and chill, or serve warm.

## Old-Fashioned Banana Pudding
> Layers of creamy vanilla pudding, ripe bananas and soft vanilla wafers.
@ Serves: 10 | Prep: 20 minutes, plus 4 hours chilling | Cook: 10 minutes
U: Vanilla Pudding Mix
- 2 cups Vanilla Pudding Mix
- 4 cups cold milk
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 box (11 ounces) vanilla wafer cookies
- 4 ripe bananas, sliced
- 2 cups sweetened whipped cream
1. Make the pudding with the mix and milk as for Homemade Vanilla Pudding, then stir in the butter and vanilla. Cool for 15 minutes.
2. In a 2-quart dish, layer wafers, banana slices and pudding. Repeat the layers, ending with pudding.
3. Cover and chill for at least 4 hours.
4. Spread with whipped cream before serving.

## Chocolate Pudding Pie
> A cool, creamy chocolate pie with a crumbly crust and whipped cream on top.
@ Serves: 8 | Prep: 15 minutes, plus 4 hours chilling | Cook: 10 minutes
U: Chocolate Pudding Mix
- 1 cup plus 2 tablespoons Chocolate Pudding Mix
- 2 cups cold milk
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 baked 9-inch pie crust or graham cracker crust
- Sweetened whipped cream, to serve
1. Whisk the pudding mix and cold milk in a saucepan. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until very thick and bubbling. Cook 1 minute more.
2. Stir in the butter and vanilla, then pour into the crust.
3. Press plastic wrap onto the surface and chill for at least 4 hours, until set.
4. Top with whipped cream before slicing.
T: Using slightly less milk than the basic pudding recipe makes a firmer filling that slices cleanly.

## Amish Apple Crisp
> Tender, cinnamon-spiced apples under a crunchy, buttery oat topping.
@ Serves: 12 | Prep: 20 minutes | Cook: 50 minutes
U: Fruit Crisp Topping Mix
+ For the filling
- 8 medium apples, peeled and sliced (about 8 cups)
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
+ For the topping
- 3 cups Fruit Crisp Topping Mix
- 3/4 cup cold butter, cut into pieces
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9 x 13-inch baking dish.
2. Toss the apples with the sugar, flour, cinnamon and lemon juice. Spread in the dish.
3. Rub the butter into the topping mix until crumbly and sprinkle over the apples.
4. Bake for 45 to 50 minutes, until golden and the apples are tender. Serve warm with ice cream.

## Peach Crisp
> Sweet, juicy peaches baked under a golden crumble. Works with fresh, frozen or canned peaches.
@ Serves: 6 | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 40 minutes
U: Fruit Crisp Topping Mix
- 5 cups sliced peaches (fresh, thawed frozen, or canned and drained)
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1 3/4 cups Fruit Crisp Topping Mix
- 1/2 cup cold butter, cut into pieces
1. Heat the oven to 375°F and grease an 8-inch square baking dish.
2. Toss the peaches with the sugar and cornstarch and spread in the dish.
3. Rub the butter into the topping mix until crumbly and sprinkle over the peaches.
4. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes, until bubbling and golden.

## Mixed Berry Crisp
> A jammy, bubbling berry dessert you can make all year with frozen fruit.
@ Serves: 6 | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 45 minutes
U: Fruit Crisp Topping Mix
- 5 cups mixed berries (fresh or frozen, not thawed)
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- 1 teaspoon grated lemon zest
- 1 3/4 cups Fruit Crisp Topping Mix
- 1/2 cup cold butter, cut into pieces
1. Heat the oven to 375°F and grease an 8-inch square baking dish.
2. Toss the berries with the sugar, cornstarch and lemon zest and spread in the dish.
3. Rub the butter into the topping mix and sprinkle over the berries.
4. Bake for 40 to 45 minutes, until the juices bubble thickly at the edges.

## Strawberry Rhubarb Crisp
> The sweet-tart taste of spring from the Amish garden.
@ Serves: 6 | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 45 minutes
U: Fruit Crisp Topping Mix
- 3 cups sliced rhubarb (1/2-inch pieces)
- 2 cups sliced strawberries
- 2/3 cup granulated sugar
- 3 tablespoons cornstarch
- 1 3/4 cups Fruit Crisp Topping Mix
- 1/2 cup cold butter, cut into pieces
1. Heat the oven to 375°F and grease an 8-inch square baking dish.
2. Toss the rhubarb and strawberries with the sugar and cornstarch and spread in the dish.
3. Rub the butter into the topping mix and sprinkle over the fruit.
4. Bake for 40 to 45 minutes, until the fruit is bubbling and the topping is golden.
