# Pantry Shopping List
* These are the staples used to make the mixes in this book. Buy them in larger bags or in bulk to save the most money. Fresh ingredients such as eggs, butter, milk, meat and produce are added when you cook.
### Flours and Grains
- All-purpose flour
- Bread flour
- Whole wheat flour
- Wheat bran
- Yellow cornmeal
- Old-fashioned rolled oats
- Quick-cooking oats
- Long-grain white rice
- Pearl barley
- Plain dry bread crumbs
- Vital wheat gluten (optional)
### Beans, Peas and Pasta
- Green and yellow split peas
- Brown lentils
- Assorted dried beans (navy, pinto, black, kidney, great northern, black-eyed peas)
- Wide egg noodles, elbow macaroni, rotini and small soup pasta
### Leaveners
- Baking powder
- Baking soda
- Cream of tartar
- Instant yeast
### Dairy Powders
- Nonfat dry milk powder
- Buttermilk powder
- Cheddar cheese powder
- Powdered coffee creamer (optional)
### Sweeteners and Baking
- Granulated sugar
- Brown sugar
- Powdered sugar
- Maple sugar (optional)
- Unsweetened cocoa powder
- Cornstarch
- Chocolate chips (semisweet, mini and white), butterscotch chips
- Raisins, dried cranberries, shredded coconut, pecans and walnuts
- Vegetable shortening
### Seasonings and Spices
- Fine salt and coarse salt
- Black pepper and white pepper
- Garlic powder, onion powder and dried minced onion
- Sweet paprika, chili powder, ground cumin, cayenne pepper and red pepper flakes
- Dried parsley, basil, oregano, thyme, sage, dill, chives and Italian seasoning
- Celery seed and celery salt
- Ground turmeric and dry mustard
- Ground cinnamon, nutmeg, cloves, ginger, allspice and pumpkin pie spice
- Nutritional yeast flakes
- Dried mushrooms
- Instant coffee granules
### Drink Mixes
- Unsweetened instant tea powder
- Orange-flavored breakfast drink powder
- Sweetened lemonade drink powder
### Containers
- Glass canning jars and lids (half-pint, pint and quart)
- Zip-top freezer bags
- Labels and a permanent marker
