# Soup & Broth Base Mixes
* Cans of condensed soup and jars of bouillon add up quickly. These homemade bases cost pennies per serving, contain no mystery ingredients and turn water into a pot of comforting soup or a creamy casserole sauce.

## Cream Soup Base Mix (Condensed Soup Substitute)
> The most useful jar in the pantry. A third of a cup replaces one can of condensed cream soup in any casserole or recipe.
@ Makes: about 3 cups (9 can equivalents) | Prep: 10 minutes
- 2 cups nonfat dry milk powder
- 3/4 cup cornstarch
- 1/4 cup Chicken-Style Bouillon Powder or chicken bouillon granules
- 2 tablespoons dried minced onion
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon dried basil
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
1. Whisk all the ingredients together in a bowl until evenly blended.
2. Store in an airtight jar and label.
= How to Use
* To replace one 10.5-ounce can of condensed cream soup: whisk 1/3 cup mix into 1 1/4 cups cold water or broth in a saucepan. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until thick and bubbly, about 3 to 5 minutes.
K: Pantry up to 6 months
T: Always start with cold liquid. Cornstarch added to hot liquid forms lumps.

## Chicken-Style Bouillon Powder
> A savory, golden broth powder made from pantry seasonings and nutritional yeast. Use it anywhere a recipe calls for chicken bouillon.
@ Makes: about 2 cups | Prep: 10 minutes
- 1 1/3 cups nutritional yeast flakes
- 3 tablespoons fine salt
- 1 tablespoon onion powder
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon dried parsley flakes
- 2 teaspoons granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/2 teaspoon dried sage
- 1/2 teaspoon celery seed
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
1. Place all the ingredients in a blender and blend into a fine powder.
2. Store in an airtight jar and label.
= How to Use
* For 1 cup of broth: stir 2 teaspoons powder into 1 cup boiling water. Adjust to taste.
K: Pantry up to 6 months
T: This powder contains no meat, so it is also suitable for vegetarian cooking. Nutritional yeast is sold in the baking or health-food aisle.

## Beef-Style Bouillon Powder
> A deep, rich broth powder for stews, gravies and vegetable beef soup.
@ Makes: about 1 3/4 cups | Prep: 10 minutes
- 1 cup nutritional yeast flakes
- 2 tablespoons dried mushroom powder (dried mushrooms ground in a blender)
- 2 tablespoons fine salt
- 2 tablespoons onion powder
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon sweet paprika
- 2 teaspoons granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon instant coffee granules (optional, for color)
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
1. Place all the ingredients in a blender and blend into a fine powder.
2. Store in an airtight jar and label.
= How to Use
* For 1 cup of broth: stir 2 teaspoons powder into 1 cup boiling water. Adjust to taste.
K: Pantry up to 6 months

## Homemade Onion Soup Mix
> Use it for onion soup, dips, pot roast and meatloaf. Each 1/4 cup replaces one envelope of store-bought mix.
@ Makes: about 1 1/4 cups (5 envelope equivalents) | Prep: 5 minutes
- 1 cup dried minced onion
- 1/3 cup Beef-Style Bouillon Powder or beef bouillon granules
- 4 teaspoons onion powder
- 1/2 teaspoon sweet paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon celery salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
1. Stir all the ingredients together.
2. Store in an airtight jar and label. Shake well before measuring.
= How to Use
* Use 1/4 cup mix in place of one 1-ounce envelope of onion soup mix. For dip, stir 2 tablespoons mix into 1 cup sour cream and chill for 1 hour.
K: Pantry up to 6 months

## Bean and Barley Soup Mix
> A filling, budget-friendly soup made from lentils, split peas and barley. No soaking is needed.
@ Makes: 1 jar (8 servings) | Prep: 10 minutes
+ For the jar
- 1/2 cup pearl barley
- 1/2 cup green split peas
- 1/2 cup yellow split peas
- 1/2 cup brown lentils
+ For the seasoning packet
- 2 tablespoons Chicken-Style Bouillon Powder
- 1 tablespoon dried minced onion
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 bay leaf
1. Layer the barley, split peas and lentils in a 1-quart jar.
2. Mix the seasoning ingredients in a small bag and place it on top of the jar contents, or tape it to the lid.
3. Repeat to make as many jars as you like.
= How to Use
* See Hearty Bean and Barley Soup for the full method.
K: Pantry up to 1 year (seasoning packet best within 6 months)

## Cream of Chicken Soup
> A creamy, comforting bowl of soup, ready in 15 minutes from your pantry base.
@ Serves: 4 | Prep: 5 minutes | Cook: 15 minutes
U: Cream Soup Base Mix (Condensed Soup Substitute)
- 2/3 cup Cream Soup Base Mix
- 2 1/2 cups cold chicken broth
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1 1/2 cups diced cooked chicken
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- Chopped fresh parsley, to serve
1. Whisk the soup base into the cold broth and milk in a saucepan until smooth.
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until thick and bubbly.
3. Stir in the chicken and heat through. Season to taste and sprinkle with parsley.

## Cream of Mushroom Soup
> Silky and full of earthy mushroom flavor. Far better than anything from a can.
@ Serves: 4 | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 20 minutes
U: Cream Soup Base Mix (Condensed Soup Substitute)
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 8 ounces mushrooms, sliced
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 2/3 cup Cream Soup Base Mix
- 2 1/2 cups cold water or broth
- 1/2 cup milk
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
1. Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the mushrooms and onion and cook until golden, about 8 minutes. Remove from the heat.
2. In a bowl, whisk the soup base into the cold water and milk.
3. Pour into the saucepan and cook over medium heat, stirring, until thick and bubbly.
4. Season with salt and pepper.

## Broccoli Cheddar Soup
> Thick, cheesy and loaded with tender broccoli. Serve it in bread bowls for a treat.
@ Serves: 6 | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 25 minutes
U: Cream Soup Base Mix (Condensed Soup Substitute), Chicken-Style Bouillon Powder
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 1 carrot, grated
- 3 cups water mixed with 2 tablespoons Chicken-Style Bouillon Powder
- 4 cups small broccoli florets
- 2/3 cup Cream Soup Base Mix
- 1 1/2 cups cold milk
- 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese
1. Melt the butter in a large pot. Cook the onion and carrot until soft, about 5 minutes.
2. Add the broth and broccoli. Simmer until the broccoli is tender, about 10 minutes.
3. Whisk the soup base into the cold milk and stir it into the pot. Cook, stirring, until thickened.
4. Remove from the heat and stir in the cheese a handful at a time until melted.

## Amish Chicken Corn Soup
> A Pennsylvania Dutch church-supper classic with chicken, sweet corn and little flour dumplings called rivels.
@ Serves: 8 | Prep: 20 minutes | Cook: 30 minutes
U: Chicken-Style Bouillon Powder
+ For the soup
- 8 cups water
- 1/3 cup Chicken-Style Bouillon Powder
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 3 cups diced cooked chicken
- 3 cups corn kernels (fresh or frozen)
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
+ For the rivels
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 large egg
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 2 hard-boiled eggs, chopped (optional, to serve)
1. Bring the water and bouillon powder to a boil in a large pot. Add the onion and celery and simmer for 10 minutes.
2. Add the chicken and corn and return to a simmer.
3. For the rivels, rub the flour, egg and salt together with your fingertips until the mixture forms small, pea-sized crumbs.
4. Drop the rivels into the simmering soup a little at a time, stirring. Cook for 10 minutes.
5. Stir in the parsley, season, and serve topped with chopped egg, if you like.

## Creamy Potato Soup
> Thick, hearty potato soup with bacon and cheese, a farmhouse favorite on cold days.
@ Serves: 6 | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 30 minutes
U: Cream Soup Base Mix (Condensed Soup Substitute), Chicken-Style Bouillon Powder
- 4 slices bacon, chopped
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 6 medium potatoes, peeled and diced
- 4 cups water mixed with 2 tablespoons plus 2 teaspoons Chicken-Style Bouillon Powder
- 2/3 cup Cream Soup Base Mix
- 1 1/2 cups cold milk
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- Sliced green onions, to serve
1. Cook the bacon in a large pot until crisp. Remove with a slotted spoon, leaving 1 tablespoon drippings.
2. Cook the onion in the drippings until soft. Add the potatoes and broth and simmer until tender, about 15 minutes.
3. Lightly mash some of the potatoes in the pot to thicken the soup.
4. Whisk the soup base into the cold milk, add to the pot and cook, stirring, until thickened.
5. Serve topped with bacon, cheese and green onions.

## French Onion Soup
> Sweet, slowly caramelized onions in a rich broth under a lid of melted cheese.
@ Serves: 4 | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 1 hour
U: Beef-Style Bouillon Powder
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 4 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 1 teaspoon granulated sugar
- 6 cups water
- 1/4 cup Beef-Style Bouillon Powder
- 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
- 4 thick slices French bread, toasted
- 1 1/2 cups shredded Swiss or Gruyère cheese
1. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium-low heat. Add the onions and sugar and cook, stirring often, until deep golden brown, 35 to 40 minutes.
2. Add the water, bouillon powder and thyme. Simmer for 15 minutes.
3. Ladle into oven-safe bowls, top each with a slice of toast and cover with cheese.
4. Broil until the cheese is bubbly and browned, 2 to 3 minutes.

## Savory Onion Pot Roast
> Fork-tender beef, potatoes and carrots in a rich onion gravy. Let the slow cooker do the work.
@ Serves: 6 to 8 | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 8 hours
U: Homemade Onion Soup Mix
- 3 to 4 pound beef chuck roast
- 1/4 cup Homemade Onion Soup Mix
- 1 cup water
- 1 1/2 pounds small potatoes, halved
- 4 carrots, cut into chunks
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch mixed with 1/4 cup cold water
1. Place the potatoes and carrots in a large slow cooker. Set the roast on top.
2. Mix the onion soup mix with the water and pour over the roast.
3. Cover and cook on low for 8 to 9 hours, until the beef is very tender.
4. Lift out the meat and vegetables. Stir the cornstarch mixture into the cooking juices and cook on high for 15 minutes, until thickened.
5. Serve the roast and vegetables with the gravy.
T: To bake in the oven instead, use a covered Dutch oven at 325°F for 3 to 3 1/2 hours.

## Hearty Bean and Barley Soup
> A thick, satisfying soup that stretches a few pantry staples into a meal for eight.
@ Serves: 8 | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 1 hour
U: Bean and Barley Soup Mix
- 1 jar Bean and Barley Soup Mix, with its seasoning packet
- 10 cups water
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 1 can (14.5 ounces) diced tomatoes
- 1 cup diced cooked ham or smoked sausage (optional)
1. Rinse the barley, peas and lentils in a strainer and pick out any debris.
2. Place in a large pot with the water, seasoning packet, carrots and celery. Bring to a boil.
3. Reduce the heat, cover and simmer for 45 minutes, stirring now and then.
4. Add the tomatoes and ham and simmer 15 minutes more, until the barley and peas are tender. Remove the bay leaf. Add more water if the soup becomes too thick.

## Vegetable Beef Soup
> Chunky, homestyle soup packed with beef and garden vegetables.
@ Serves: 8 | Prep: 20 minutes | Cook: 1 hour 30 minutes
U: Beef-Style Bouillon Powder
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 1 pound beef stew meat, cut into small cubes
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 8 cups water
- 1/3 cup Beef-Style Bouillon Powder
- 1 can (14.5 ounces) diced tomatoes
- 3 medium potatoes, diced
- 3 carrots, sliced
- 1 cup green beans, cut into pieces
- 1 cup corn kernels
- 1 cup peas
1. Heat the oil in a large pot and brown the beef on all sides. Add the onion and cook 3 minutes.
2. Add the water, bouillon powder and tomatoes. Cover and simmer for 1 hour, until the beef is tender.
3. Add the potatoes, carrots and green beans and simmer for 20 minutes.
4. Stir in the corn and peas and cook 5 minutes more. Season to taste.

## Homestyle Chicken Noodle Soup
> Golden broth, tender chicken and plenty of egg noodles. The cure for whatever ails you.
@ Serves: 6 | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 30 minutes
U: Chicken-Style Bouillon Powder
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 2 celery stalks, sliced
- 8 cups water
- 1/3 cup Chicken-Style Bouillon Powder
- 3 cups wide egg noodles
- 3 cups diced cooked chicken
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
1. Melt the butter in a large pot. Cook the onion, carrots and celery until softened, about 6 minutes.
2. Add the water and bouillon powder and bring to a boil.
3. Add the noodles and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, until tender.
4. Stir in the chicken and parsley and heat through. Season to taste.
