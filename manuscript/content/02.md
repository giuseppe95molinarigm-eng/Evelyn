# Pancake, Waffle & Breakfast Mixes
* Make-ahead mornings start here. With the dry ingredients measured and waiting in a jar, a stack of pancakes, a pan of baked oatmeal or a warm bowl of oats is only a stir away, even on the busiest school day.

## Old-Fashioned Buttermilk Pancake Mix
> Light, fluffy and lightly tangy, just like the pancakes served at a Sunday breakfast after chores.
@ Makes: about 8 cups (5 batches) | Prep: 10 minutes
- 6 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup buttermilk powder
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons baking powder
- 1 tablespoon baking soda
- 1 tablespoon salt
1. Whisk all the ingredients together in a large bowl, breaking up any clumps of buttermilk powder.
2. Store in an airtight container and label.
= How to Use
* For 10 to 12 pancakes: whisk 1 1/2 cups mix with 1 egg, 1 1/4 cups water and 2 tablespoons vegetable oil or melted butter just until combined. A few lumps are fine.
K: Pantry up to 3 months; refrigerator up to 6 months

## Whole Grain Oat Pancake Mix
> Hearty, wholesome pancakes made with whole wheat flour and oats, lightly sweetened and warmly spiced.
@ Makes: about 8 cups (5 batches) | Prep: 10 minutes
- 3 cups whole wheat flour
- 3 cups quick-cooking oats
- 1 cup nonfat dry milk powder
- 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
- 3 tablespoons baking powder
- 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
- 2 teaspoons salt
1. Whisk all the ingredients together in a large bowl, pressing out any lumps of brown sugar.
2. Store in an airtight container and label.
= How to Use
* For about 10 pancakes: whisk 1 1/2 cups mix with 1 egg, 1 cup water and 2 tablespoons vegetable oil. Let the batter rest for 5 minutes so the oats can soften before cooking.
K: Pantry up to 1 month; refrigerator up to 3 months
T: For smoother pancakes, pulse the oats in a blender a few times before mixing.

## Crispy Waffle Mix
> A little cornstarch is the secret to waffles that are crisp on the outside and fluffy inside.
@ Makes: about 8 cups (4 batches) | Prep: 10 minutes
- 6 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup nonfat dry milk powder
- 1/2 cup cornstarch
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 3 tablespoons baking powder
- 1 tablespoon salt
1. Whisk all the ingredients together in a large bowl until evenly blended.
2. Store in an airtight container and label.
= How to Use
* For 4 to 5 large waffles: whisk 2 cups mix with 2 eggs, 1 1/2 cups water, 1/2 cup melted butter or vegetable oil and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract.
K: Pantry up to 3 months; refrigerator up to 6 months

## Amish Baked Oatmeal Mix
> Baked oatmeal is a beloved Amish breakfast: soft and custardy in the middle, golden on top. Each bag makes one full pan.
@ Makes: 4 bags (1 pan each) | Prep: 15 minutes
- 12 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
- 3 cups packed brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons plus 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 4 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 2 teaspoons salt
1. In a very large bowl, mix all the ingredients well, breaking up the brown sugar with your fingers.
2. Divide evenly among 4 zip-top bags or jars (about 4 cups each). Label each one with the baking directions below.
= How to Use
* For one 9 x 13-inch pan: stir 1 bag of mix with 1 cup milk, 2 eggs, 1/2 cup melted butter and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract. Spread in a greased pan and bake at 350°F for 30 to 35 minutes.
K: Pantry up to 6 months
T: If the brown sugar hardens in storage, simply break it up when you mix the batter.

## Maple Brown Sugar Instant Oatmeal Mix
> Skip the expensive single-serve packets. This homemade version is ready in two minutes and you choose the sweetness.
@ Makes: about 10 cups (20 servings) | Prep: 10 minutes
- 8 cups quick-cooking oats
- 1 cup nonfat dry milk powder
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/2 cup maple sugar (or additional brown sugar)
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon salt
1. Place 2 cups of the oats in a blender and pulse into a coarse powder. This makes the finished oatmeal creamier.
2. In a large bowl, combine the powdered oats with the remaining oats and all the other ingredients. Mix well.
3. Store in an airtight container, or portion 1/2 cup into small bags for grab-and-go breakfasts.
= How to Use
* For 1 serving: stir 1/2 cup mix with 2/3 cup boiling water. Cover and let stand for 2 minutes. Or microwave with 2/3 cup cold water for 1 to 1 1/2 minutes, stirring once.
K: Pantry up to 6 months

## Fluffy Buttermilk Pancakes
> Golden, tender pancakes in the time it takes to heat the griddle.
@ Makes: 10 to 12 pancakes | Prep: 5 minutes | Cook: 15 minutes
U: Old-Fashioned Buttermilk Pancake Mix
- 1 1/2 cups Old-Fashioned Buttermilk Pancake Mix
- 1 large egg
- 1 1/4 cups water
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil or melted butter
- Butter and maple syrup, to serve
1. Heat a griddle or large skillet over medium heat and grease it lightly.
2. Whisk the egg, water and oil in a bowl. Add the pancake mix and stir just until combined. Let the batter rest for 2 minutes.
3. Pour 1/4 cup batter per pancake onto the griddle. Cook until bubbles form on top and the edges look set, about 2 minutes.
4. Flip and cook 1 to 2 minutes more, until golden. Serve with butter and syrup.
T: Overmixing makes tough pancakes. Stop stirring as soon as no dry flour remains.

## Blueberry Pancakes
> Bursting with sweet berries in every bite.
@ Makes: 10 to 12 pancakes | Prep: 5 minutes | Cook: 15 minutes
U: Old-Fashioned Buttermilk Pancake Mix
- 1 1/2 cups Old-Fashioned Buttermilk Pancake Mix
- 1 large egg
- 1 1/4 cups water
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil or melted butter
- 1 cup fresh or frozen blueberries (do not thaw)
1. Heat a greased griddle over medium heat.
2. Mix the batter as for Fluffy Buttermilk Pancakes.
3. Pour 1/4 cup batter onto the griddle, then scatter a few blueberries over each pancake.
4. Cook until bubbles form, flip and cook until golden.
T: Adding the berries on the griddle instead of into the bowl keeps the batter from turning purple.

## Apple Cinnamon Oat Pancakes
> Hearty, cozy pancakes with grated apple and cinnamon, perfect for a crisp fall morning.
@ Makes: about 10 pancakes | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 15 minutes
U: Whole Grain Oat Pancake Mix
- 1 1/2 cups Whole Grain Oat Pancake Mix
- 1 large egg
- 1 cup water
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 medium apple, peeled and grated
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
1. Whisk the egg, water and oil. Stir in the pancake mix, grated apple and cinnamon. Let stand for 5 minutes.
2. Heat a greased griddle over medium heat.
3. Pour 1/4 cup batter per pancake. Cook until the edges are set, about 2 to 3 minutes, then flip and cook until golden.
4. Serve with maple syrup or warm applesauce.

## Golden Crispy Waffles
> Crisp, golden and light as air, with deep pockets for butter and syrup.
@ Makes: 4 to 5 large waffles | Prep: 5 minutes | Cook: 20 minutes
U: Crispy Waffle Mix
- 2 cups Crispy Waffle Mix
- 2 large eggs
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1/2 cup melted butter or vegetable oil
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
1. Heat the waffle iron and grease it lightly.
2. Whisk the eggs, water, butter and vanilla. Stir in the waffle mix just until combined.
3. Pour the batter into the iron according to the manufacturer’s directions and cook until golden and crisp.
4. Keep finished waffles warm in a single layer on a rack in a 200°F oven while you cook the rest.

## Sheet Pan Pancakes
> Feed the whole family at once, with no standing at the griddle. Top each section differently so everyone gets a favorite.
@ Serves: 6 to 8 | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 15 minutes
U: Old-Fashioned Buttermilk Pancake Mix
- 3 cups Old-Fashioned Buttermilk Pancake Mix
- 2 large eggs
- 2 1/2 cups water
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil or melted butter
- Toppings: blueberries, sliced strawberries, chocolate chips or cooked crumbled bacon
1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Grease an 18 x 13-inch rimmed baking sheet and line it with parchment paper.
2. Whisk the eggs, water and oil, then stir in the pancake mix.
3. Spread the batter evenly in the pan and scatter your toppings over it.
4. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.
5. Cut into squares and serve with syrup.

## Amish Baked Oatmeal with Berries
> Soft and custardy with jammy pockets of fruit. Leftovers reheat beautifully all week.
@ Serves: 8 | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 35 minutes
U: Amish Baked Oatmeal Mix
- 1 bag Amish Baked Oatmeal Mix
- 1 cup milk
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup melted butter
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 1/2 cups fresh or frozen mixed berries
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9 x 13-inch baking dish.
2. In a large bowl, whisk the milk, eggs, butter and vanilla. Stir in the oatmeal mix.
3. Fold in the berries and spread in the baking dish.
4. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until golden and set in the center.
5. Serve warm, with a splash of milk poured over each serving the Amish way.

## Peanut Butter Banana Baked Oatmeal
> A filling, kid-friendly twist on baked oatmeal that tastes like a peanut butter and banana sandwich.
@ Serves: 8 | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 35 minutes
U: Amish Baked Oatmeal Mix
- 1 bag Amish Baked Oatmeal Mix
- 3/4 cup milk
- 2 large eggs
- 2 ripe bananas, mashed
- 1/2 cup creamy peanut butter
- 1/4 cup melted butter
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9 x 13-inch baking dish.
2. Whisk the milk, eggs, bananas, peanut butter, butter and vanilla until smooth.
3. Stir in the oatmeal mix and spread in the dish.
4. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until set and golden.
T: Top with banana slices and a drizzle of honey just before serving.

## Sausage Pancake Muffins
> Pancake and sausage baked together in a muffin cup: a handheld breakfast for busy mornings.
@ Makes: 12 muffins | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 18 minutes
U: Old-Fashioned Buttermilk Pancake Mix
- 1/2 pound bulk breakfast sausage, cooked, crumbled and drained
- 1 1/2 cups Old-Fashioned Buttermilk Pancake Mix
- 1 large egg
- 1 cup water
- 2 tablespoons maple syrup, plus more to serve
- 2 tablespoons melted butter
1. Heat the oven to 375°F and grease a 12-cup muffin pan.
2. Whisk the egg, water, syrup and butter. Stir in the pancake mix, then fold in the sausage.
3. Divide the batter among the muffin cups.
4. Bake for 15 to 18 minutes, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.
5. Serve warm with syrup for dipping.

## Chocolate Chip Waffles
> A special-occasion breakfast for birthdays and snow days.
@ Makes: 4 to 5 large waffles | Prep: 5 minutes | Cook: 20 minutes
U: Crispy Waffle Mix
- 2 cups Crispy Waffle Mix
- 2 large eggs
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1/2 cup melted butter
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 3/4 cup mini chocolate chips
1. Heat and grease the waffle iron.
2. Mix the batter as for Golden Crispy Waffles, then fold in the chocolate chips.
3. Cook in the waffle iron until golden.
4. Serve with whipped cream and sliced strawberries.

## Apple Pie Overnight Oats
> Stir it together at night and wake up to a creamy, ready-to-eat breakfast that tastes like apple pie.
@ Serves: 1 | Prep: 5 minutes, plus overnight chilling
U: Maple Brown Sugar Instant Oatmeal Mix
- 1/2 cup Maple Brown Sugar Instant Oatmeal Mix
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/4 cup plain or vanilla yogurt
- 1/4 cup unsweetened applesauce
- 1/4 teaspoon apple pie spice
- 1/4 cup diced apple
- 1 tablespoon chopped walnuts or pecans (optional)
1. In a jar or container, stir together the oatmeal mix, milk, yogurt, applesauce and apple pie spice.
2. Top with the diced apple. Cover and refrigerate overnight, or for at least 4 hours.
3. Stir, sprinkle with nuts and enjoy cold, or warm it in the microwave for 1 minute.
