# Seasoning Blends & Dressing Mixes
* A few big-batch spice blends can transform plain chicken, beef, potatoes and vegetables into family favorites. Mixing your own costs far less than buying individual packets, and you control the salt and the heat.

## Homemade Taco Seasoning
> Bold, warm and just spicy enough. Two tablespoons replace one store-bought packet.
@ Makes: about 3/4 cup (6 packet equivalents) | Prep: 5 minutes
- 1/4 cup chili powder
- 2 tablespoons ground cumin
- 1 tablespoon sweet paprika
- 1 tablespoon onion powder
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 2 teaspoons cornstarch
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
1. Stir all the ingredients together until evenly blended.
2. Store in an airtight jar and label.
= How to Use
* For 1 pound of ground meat: brown and drain the meat, then stir in 2 tablespoons seasoning and 1/2 cup water. Simmer until thickened, about 5 minutes.
K: Pantry up to 6 months

## Ranch Dressing and Dip Mix
> Cool, herby ranch for dressings, dips, potatoes and chicken.
@ Makes: about 2/3 cup | Prep: 5 minutes
- 1/2 cup buttermilk powder
- 1 tablespoon dried parsley flakes
- 2 teaspoons onion powder
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon dried dill weed
- 1 teaspoon dried minced onion
- 1 teaspoon dried chives
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
1. Stir all the ingredients together. For a finer texture, pulse briefly in a blender.
2. Store in an airtight jar and label.
= How to Use
* Dressing: whisk 2 tablespoons mix with 1/2 cup mayonnaise and 1/2 cup milk. Chill for 30 minutes.
* Dip: stir 2 tablespoons mix into 1 cup sour cream. Chill for 30 minutes.
K: Pantry up to 3 months; refrigerator up to 6 months

## Italian Dressing Mix
> Zesty and herby, for salad dressings, marinades and roasted vegetables.
@ Makes: about 2/3 cup | Prep: 5 minutes
- 2 tablespoons dried oregano
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon onion powder
- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon dried parsley flakes
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 1 teaspoon dried basil
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1/4 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/4 teaspoon celery salt
1. Stir all the ingredients together.
2. Store in an airtight jar and label.
= How to Use
* For about 1 cup of dressing: shake 2 tablespoons mix with 1/4 cup vinegar and 2 tablespoons water in a jar until the sugar and salt dissolve. Add 2/3 cup olive or vegetable oil and shake again.
K: Pantry up to 6 months

## Seasoned Coating Mix for Oven-Baked Meats
> A crispy, golden coating for chicken and pork that bakes up crunchy without frying.
@ Makes: about 3 cups | Prep: 5 minutes
- 2 cups plain dry bread crumbs
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons sweet paprika
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons onion powder
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/2 teaspoon granulated sugar
1. Stir all the ingredients together.
2. Store in an airtight container and label.
= How to Use
* Use about 1 cup mix for 2 to 3 pounds of chicken pieces, or 2/3 cup for 4 pork chops. Shake only what you need into a bag. Discard any mix that has touched raw meat.
K: Pantry up to 3 months

## Amish Seasoning Salt
> A savory, all-purpose seasoning salt for eggs, potatoes, burgers, vegetables and fries.
@ Makes: about 3/4 cup | Prep: 5 minutes
- 1/2 cup fine salt
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons sweet paprika
- 1 tablespoon onion powder
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
1. Stir all the ingredients together, or pulse in a blender for an even blend.
2. Store in a shaker jar and label.
= How to Use
* Sprinkle on meats, potatoes and vegetables instead of plain salt.
K: Pantry up to 1 year

## Beef Taco Skillet
> Seasoned beef, beans, corn and rice cooked together in one pan and finished with melted cheese.
@ Serves: 6 | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 30 minutes
U: Homemade Taco Seasoning
- 1 pound ground beef
- 2 tablespoons Homemade Taco Seasoning
- 1 can (14.5 ounces) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (15 ounces) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup corn kernels
- 1 cup long-grain white rice (uncooked)
- 1 3/4 cups water
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
1. Brown the beef in a large, deep skillet and drain.
2. Stir in the taco seasoning, tomatoes, beans, corn, rice and water. Bring to a boil.
3. Reduce the heat, cover and simmer for 18 to 20 minutes, until the rice is tender.
4. Sprinkle with cheese, cover and let stand until melted. Serve with sour cream and tortilla chips.

## Easy Taco Soup
> A dump-and-simmer soup that tastes like it cooked all day. Perfect for game days and potlucks.
@ Serves: 8 | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 30 minutes
U: Homemade Taco Seasoning, Ranch Dressing and Dip Mix
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 tablespoons Homemade Taco Seasoning
- 2 tablespoons Ranch Dressing and Dip Mix
- 2 cans (15 ounces each) pinto or kidney beans, undrained
- 1 can (15 ounces) corn, undrained
- 2 cans (14.5 ounces each) diced tomatoes
- 2 cups water
1. Brown the beef and onion in a large pot and drain.
2. Stir in all the remaining ingredients and bring to a boil.
3. Reduce the heat and simmer for 20 minutes.
4. Serve topped with cheese, sour cream and crushed tortilla chips.

## Creamy Ranch Dip
> The party dip everyone asks for, with fresh vegetables, chips or pretzels.
@ Makes: about 1 1/4 cups | Prep: 5 minutes, plus 30 minutes chilling
U: Ranch Dressing and Dip Mix
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 2 tablespoons Ranch Dressing and Dip Mix
1. Stir all the ingredients together until smooth.
2. Cover and chill for at least 30 minutes so the herbs soften and the flavors blend.
3. Serve with fresh vegetables, crackers or chips.

## Ranch Roasted Potatoes
> Crispy-edged potatoes coated in tangy ranch seasoning.
@ Serves: 6 | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 35 minutes
U: Ranch Dressing and Dip Mix
- 2 pounds baby potatoes, halved
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons Ranch Dressing and Dip Mix
- Chopped fresh parsley, to serve
1. Heat the oven to 425°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Toss the potatoes with the oil and ranch mix.
3. Spread in a single layer, cut sides down.
4. Roast for 30 to 35 minutes, until golden and tender, turning once. Sprinkle with parsley.

## Crispy Oven-Baked Chicken
> Golden, crunchy chicken with juicy meat inside and no frying needed.
@ Serves: 4 to 6 | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 50 minutes
U: Seasoned Coating Mix for Oven-Baked Meats
- 3 pounds bone-in chicken pieces
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1 cup Seasoned Coating Mix
- 3 tablespoons melted butter or vegetable oil
1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with foil and set a greased rack on top.
2. Dip the chicken pieces in milk, then shake them in a bag with the coating mix.
3. Arrange on the rack and drizzle with the melted butter.
4. Bake for 45 to 50 minutes, until golden and the thickest pieces reach 165°F inside.

## Italian Pasta Salad
> A colorful, make-ahead salad for picnics, potlucks and summer suppers.
@ Serves: 10 | Prep: 20 minutes, plus 1 hour chilling | Cook: 10 minutes
U: Italian Dressing Mix
- 1 pound rotini pasta
- 1 batch dressing made from Italian Dressing Mix (about 1 cup)
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 can (6 ounces) sliced black olives, drained
- 4 ounces salami, diced
- 1 cup small mozzarella balls or cubes
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
1. Cook the pasta in salted water until just tender. Drain and rinse with cold water.
2. Toss the pasta with all the remaining ingredients.
3. Cover and chill for at least 1 hour. Stir before serving.

## Zesty Italian Chicken
> Juicy, herb-marinated chicken breasts for baking or grilling.
@ Serves: 4 | Prep: 10 minutes, plus 30 minutes marinating | Cook: 25 minutes
U: Italian Dressing Mix
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 2 tablespoons Italian Dressing Mix
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar or lemon juice
1. Whisk the dressing mix, oil and vinegar in a bowl or zip-top bag.
2. Add the chicken, turn to coat and refrigerate for 30 minutes to 4 hours.
3. Heat the oven to 400°F. Place the chicken in a baking dish and discard the marinade.
4. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until the chicken reaches 165°F inside. Rest 5 minutes before slicing.
T: To grill, cook over medium-high heat for 6 to 7 minutes per side.

## Seasoned Pork Chops
> Crisp-coated, tender pork chops ready in about 20 minutes.
@ Serves: 4 | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 22 minutes
U: Seasoned Coating Mix for Oven-Baked Meats
- 4 boneless pork chops, 3/4 to 1 inch thick
- 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard or 1/4 cup milk
- 2/3 cup Seasoned Coating Mix
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Brush the chops with mustard, then press them into the coating mix to cover both sides.
3. Place on the baking sheet and drizzle with oil.
4. Bake for 18 to 22 minutes, until golden and the pork reaches 145°F inside. Rest for 3 minutes.

## Seasoned Oven Fries
> Crispy, golden fries baked in the oven with a sprinkle of seasoning salt.
@ Serves: 4 | Prep: 10 minutes, plus 30 minutes soaking | Cook: 35 minutes
U: Amish Seasoning Salt
- 4 medium russet potatoes, cut into 1/2-inch sticks
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 2 teaspoons Amish Seasoning Salt
1. Soak the potato sticks in cold water for 30 minutes. Drain and pat completely dry.
2. Heat the oven to 425°F and line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
3. Toss the potatoes with the oil and seasoning salt and spread them in a single layer.
4. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, flipping halfway, until crisp and golden.

## Homemade Seasoned Croutons
> Crunchy, flavorful croutons for salads and soups. A thrifty way to use up day-old bread.
@ Makes: about 6 cups | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 20 minutes
U: Italian Dressing Mix
- 6 cups day-old bread, cut into 3/4-inch cubes
- 1/3 cup olive oil or melted butter
- 1 tablespoon Italian Dressing Mix
- 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese
1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. Toss the bread cubes with the oil, dressing mix and Parmesan until evenly coated.
3. Spread on a baking sheet and bake for 15 to 20 minutes, stirring once, until golden and crisp.
4. Cool completely and store in an airtight container for up to 1 week.
