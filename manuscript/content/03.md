# Quick Bread, Muffin & Cornbread Mixes
* No yeast and no waiting. These mixes rely on baking powder and baking soda, so tender loaves, muffins and cornbread go from jar to oven in minutes, ready for breakfast, lunch boxes and supper alike.

## Amish Cornbread Mix
> Lightly sweet, golden cornbread is a must beside a bowl of chili or beans. This mix makes four pans.
@ Makes: about 10 cups (4 batches) | Prep: 10 minutes
- 4 cups yellow cornmeal
- 4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup nonfat dry milk powder
- 1/4 cup baking powder
- 1 tablespoon salt
1. Whisk all the ingredients together in a very large bowl.
2. Store in an airtight container and label.
= How to Use
* For one 8-inch pan: stir 2 1/2 cups mix with 1 egg, 1 cup water and 1/3 cup vegetable oil or melted butter. Bake at 400°F for 20 to 25 minutes.
K: Pantry up to 3 months; refrigerator or freezer up to 6 months

## Basic Muffin Mix
> One plain, sweet base and endless variations: add berries, apples, chocolate chips or nuts.
@ Makes: about 12 cups (4 batches of 12 muffins) | Prep: 10 minutes
- 8 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 2/3 cups granulated sugar
- 1 cup nonfat dry milk powder
- 1/4 cup baking powder
- 2 teaspoons salt
1. Whisk all the ingredients together in a very large bowl until evenly mixed.
2. Store in an airtight container and label.
= How to Use
* For 12 muffins: whisk 1 egg, 1 cup water, 1/3 cup vegetable oil and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract. Stir in 3 cups mix just until moistened. Bake at 400°F for 18 to 20 minutes.
K: Pantry up to 3 months

## Amish Cinnamon Bread Mix (No Starter)
> Amish cinnamon bread is usually made from a sourdough-style starter that takes ten days to tend. This mix gives you the same sweet, cinnamon-swirled loaves without the wait.
@ Makes: 3 bags (2 loaves each) | Prep: 10 minutes
+ For each bag
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup nonfat dry milk powder
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
1. For each bag, whisk the ingredients together in a bowl and pour into a zip-top bag or jar. Repeat to make 3 bags.
2. In a small separate bag, mix 1/3 cup granulated sugar with 1 1/2 teaspoons cinnamon for the cinnamon-sugar coating, and tuck it in with each bag of mix.
= How to Use
* See Amish Cinnamon Bread in this chapter for the full method.
K: Pantry up to 3 months

## Spiced Quick Bread Mix
> One warmly spiced mix that becomes banana bread, zucchini bread or pumpkin bread, depending on what is in season.
@ Makes: about 11 cups (4 loaves) | Prep: 10 minutes
- 7 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 2 cups packed brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons ground cinnamon
- 4 teaspoons baking soda
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cloves
1. Whisk the flour, sugars, spices, baking soda, baking powder and salt together, pressing out any lumps of brown sugar.
2. Store in an airtight container and label.
= How to Use
* For one 9 x 5-inch loaf: whisk 2 eggs, 1/2 cup vegetable oil and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract, then add your fruit or vegetable and 2 3/4 cups mix. Bake at 350°F for 55 to 65 minutes.
K: Pantry up to 3 months
T: Stir the mix before measuring, because the brown sugar can settle.

## Bran Muffin Mix
> Moist, hearty bran muffins sweetened with molasses: an old-fashioned breakfast that keeps you going until lunch.
@ Makes: about 9 cups (3 batches of 12 muffins) | Prep: 10 minutes
- 3 cups wheat bran
- 3 cups whole wheat flour
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 1 cup nonfat dry milk powder
- 1 tablespoon baking soda
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
- 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
1. Whisk all the ingredients together in a large bowl.
2. Store in an airtight container and label.
= How to Use
* For 12 muffins: whisk 1 egg, 1 cup water, 1/4 cup vegetable oil and 1/4 cup molasses. Stir in 3 cups mix and let stand 5 minutes. Bake at 375°F for 18 to 20 minutes.
K: Pantry up to 1 month; refrigerator or freezer up to 6 months

## Skillet Cornbread
> Baked in a sizzling hot cast-iron skillet for crisp, buttery edges.
@ Serves: 8 | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 22 minutes
U: Amish Cornbread Mix
- 2 tablespoons butter, for the skillet
- 2 1/2 cups Amish Cornbread Mix
- 1 large egg
- 1 cup buttermilk or water
- 1/3 cup melted butter
1. Place the 2 tablespoons butter in a 10-inch cast-iron skillet. Put the skillet in the oven and heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Whisk the egg, buttermilk and melted butter. Stir in the cornbread mix just until combined.
3. Carefully remove the hot skillet, swirl the melted butter to coat, and pour in the batter. It should sizzle.
4. Bake for 18 to 22 minutes, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean. Cut into wedges.

## Sweet Honey Corn Muffins
> Tender, sweet muffins with a drizzle of honey baked right in.
@ Makes: 12 muffins | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 16 minutes
U: Amish Cornbread Mix
- 2 1/2 cups Amish Cornbread Mix
- 1 large egg
- 1 cup water
- 1/3 cup melted butter
- 1/4 cup honey, plus more to serve
1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a 12-cup muffin pan with paper liners.
2. Whisk the egg, water, butter and honey. Stir in the cornbread mix just until moistened.
3. Divide among the muffin cups.
4. Bake for 15 to 18 minutes, until golden. Serve warm with honey butter.

## Cornbread-Topped Chili Bake
> Hearty beef and bean chili baked under a golden cornbread crust: a complete one-pan supper.
@ Serves: 6 | Prep: 20 minutes | Cook: 25 minutes
U: Amish Cornbread Mix
+ For the chili
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 1 can (15 ounces) kidney or pinto beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 can (14.5 ounces) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (8 ounces) tomato sauce
- 2 tablespoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
+ For the topping
- 2 1/2 cups Amish Cornbread Mix
- 1 large egg
- 1 cup water
- 1/3 cup vegetable oil
1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
2. In a 10- or 12-inch oven-safe skillet, cook the beef and onion over medium-high heat until browned. Drain the fat.
3. Stir in the beans, tomatoes, tomato sauce, chili powder, cumin and salt. Simmer for 10 minutes.
4. Stir together the cornbread mix, egg, water and oil. Spoon the batter evenly over the hot chili.
5. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until the topping is golden and a toothpick inserted in it comes out clean.

## Blueberry Streusel Muffins
> Bakery-style muffins with juicy blueberries and a crunchy cinnamon crumb topping.
@ Makes: 12 muffins | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 20 minutes
U: Basic Muffin Mix
+ For the muffins
- 3 cups Basic Muffin Mix
- 1 large egg
- 1 cup water
- 1/3 cup vegetable oil
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 1/2 cups fresh or frozen blueberries
+ For the streusel
- 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/3 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 3 tablespoons cold butter
1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a 12-cup muffin pan with paper liners.
2. For the streusel, mix the flour, brown sugar and cinnamon, then rub in the butter until crumbly.
3. Whisk the egg, water, oil and vanilla. Stir in the muffin mix just until moistened, then fold in the blueberries.
4. Fill the muffin cups and sprinkle with streusel.
5. Bake for 18 to 22 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.

## Apple Cinnamon Muffins
> Soft, spiced muffins studded with tender apple pieces.
@ Makes: 12 muffins | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 20 minutes
U: Basic Muffin Mix
- 3 cups Basic Muffin Mix
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 large egg
- 1 cup water
- 1/3 cup vegetable oil
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 1/2 cups peeled, diced apples
- 2 tablespoons coarse or granulated sugar, for topping
1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a 12-cup muffin pan.
2. Stir the cinnamon into the muffin mix.
3. Whisk the egg, water, oil and vanilla. Stir in the muffin mix just until moistened, then fold in the apples.
4. Fill the muffin cups and sprinkle the tops with sugar.
5. Bake for 18 to 22 minutes, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.

## Amish Cinnamon Bread
> Two sweet loaves with a crackly cinnamon-sugar crust. Keep one and share the other with a neighbor.
@ Makes: 2 loaves | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 55 minutes
U: Amish Cinnamon Bread Mix (No Starter)
- 1 bag Amish Cinnamon Bread Mix, with its cinnamon-sugar packet
- 3 large eggs
- 1 cup buttermilk (or 1 cup milk mixed with 1 tablespoon lemon juice)
- 2/3 cup vegetable oil
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
1. Heat the oven to 325°F. Grease two 8 x 4-inch loaf pans.
2. Sprinkle about half of the cinnamon-sugar into the pans and tilt to coat the bottoms and sides.
3. Whisk the eggs, buttermilk, oil and vanilla. Stir in the bread mix until just combined.
4. Divide the batter between the pans and sprinkle the remaining cinnamon-sugar over the tops.
5. Bake for 50 to 60 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
6. Cool in the pans for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack.

## Classic Banana Bread
> The best use for spotty, overripe bananas.
@ Makes: 1 loaf | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 60 minutes
U: Spiced Quick Bread Mix
- 2 3/4 cups Spiced Quick Bread Mix
- 3 very ripe bananas, mashed (about 1 1/4 cups)
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil or melted butter
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup chopped walnuts (optional)
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9 x 5-inch loaf pan.
2. Whisk the bananas, eggs, oil and vanilla. Stir in the bread mix just until combined, then fold in the nuts.
3. Pour into the pan and bake for 55 to 65 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
4. Cool for 10 minutes in the pan before turning out.

## Zucchini Bread
> A moist, spiced loaf that puts a garden full of zucchini to good use.
@ Makes: 1 loaf | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 60 minutes
U: Spiced Quick Bread Mix
- 2 3/4 cups Spiced Quick Bread Mix
- 1 1/2 cups grated zucchini, lightly squeezed
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup chopped pecans or raisins (optional)
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9 x 5-inch loaf pan.
2. Whisk the eggs, oil and vanilla, then stir in the zucchini.
3. Add the bread mix and stir just until combined. Fold in the pecans or raisins.
4. Pour into the pan and bake for 55 to 65 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
T: Squeeze the zucchini gently in a clean towel. Leave it slightly moist; that moisture keeps the bread tender.

## Pumpkin Bread
> Warmly spiced and deeply moist, this is the smell of autumn in an Amish kitchen.
@ Makes: 1 loaf | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 65 minutes
U: Spiced Quick Bread Mix
- 2 3/4 cups Spiced Quick Bread Mix
- 1 cup canned pumpkin puree (not pumpkin pie filling)
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 1/3 cup water
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9 x 5-inch loaf pan.
2. Whisk the pumpkin, eggs, oil, water and vanilla until smooth.
3. Stir in the bread mix just until combined.
4. Pour into the pan and bake for 60 to 65 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.

## Morning Glory Bran Muffins
> Packed with carrot, apple, raisins and coconut, these muffins are a whole breakfast in one.
@ Makes: 12 muffins | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 20 minutes
U: Bran Muffin Mix
- 3 cups Bran Muffin Mix
- 1 large egg
- 1 cup water
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil
- 1/4 cup molasses or honey
- 1/2 cup grated carrot
- 1/2 cup grated apple
- 1/3 cup raisins
- 1/4 cup sweetened shredded coconut
1. Heat the oven to 375°F and line a 12-cup muffin pan.
2. Whisk the egg, water, oil and molasses. Stir in the bran muffin mix and let stand for 5 minutes.
3. Fold in the carrot, apple, raisins and coconut.
4. Fill the muffin cups and bake for 18 to 22 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
