# Cake, Brownie & Cookie Mixes
* Better-than-the-box desserts, made with ingredients you can pronounce. Each mix is portioned into bags that equal one boxed mix, so you can whip up a sheet cake for a church supper, a pan of brownies or a batch of cookies at a moment’s notice.

## Homemade Yellow Cake Mix
> A buttery, tender yellow cake that bakes up tall and golden. Each bag replaces one boxed cake mix.
@ Makes: 3 bags (1 cake each) | Prep: 10 minutes
+ For each bag
- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
1. For each bag, whisk the ingredients together until evenly combined and pour into a zip-top bag or jar. Repeat to make 3 bags.
2. Label each bag with the baking directions below.
= How to Use
* For one 9 x 13-inch cake: beat 1 bag of mix with 1/2 cup softened butter, 1 cup milk, 3 eggs and 2 teaspoons vanilla extract for 2 minutes. Bake at 350°F for 25 to 30 minutes.
K: Pantry up to 6 months

## Homemade Chocolate Cake Mix
> Rich, moist and deeply chocolatey. Each bag makes one sheet cake, two layers or 24 cupcakes.
@ Makes: 3 bags (1 cake each) | Prep: 10 minutes
+ For each bag
- 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 3/4 cups granulated sugar
- 3/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
1. For each bag, sift or whisk the ingredients together so there are no cocoa lumps. Pour into a zip-top bag or jar. Repeat to make 3 bags.
2. Label each bag with the baking directions below.
= How to Use
* For one 9 x 13-inch cake: beat 1 bag of mix with 2 eggs, 1 cup milk, 1/2 cup vegetable oil and 2 teaspoons vanilla extract. Stir in 1 cup hot water or hot coffee; the batter will be thin. Bake at 350°F for 30 to 35 minutes.
K: Pantry up to 6 months

## Fudgy Brownie Mix
> Dense, chewy, crackly-topped brownies with no box required.
@ Makes: 4 bags (one 8-inch pan each) | Prep: 10 minutes
+ For each bag
- 1 1/4 cups granulated sugar
- 2/3 cup all-purpose flour
- 2/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt

1. For each bag, whisk the ingredients together, breaking up any cocoa lumps. Pour into a zip-top bag or jar. Repeat to make 4 bags.
= How to Use
* For one 8-inch square pan: stir 1 bag of mix with 2/3 cup melted butter, 2 eggs and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract. Bake at 350°F for 25 to 30 minutes.
K: Pantry up to 6 months

## Yellow Sheet Cake with Chocolate Frosting
> A classic birthday and potluck cake: buttery yellow cake under a thick layer of fudgy frosting.
@ Serves: 16 | Prep: 20 minutes | Cook: 30 minutes
U: Homemade Yellow Cake Mix
+ For the cake
- 1 bag Homemade Yellow Cake Mix
- 1/2 cup butter, softened
- 1 cup milk
- 3 large eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
+ For the frosting
- 1/2 cup butter, softened
- 2/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 3 cups powdered sugar
- 1/3 cup milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a 9 x 13-inch pan.
2. Beat the cake mix, butter, milk, eggs and vanilla on low speed until moistened, then on medium for 2 minutes.
3. Spread in the pan and bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean. Cool completely.
4. For the frosting, beat the butter and cocoa until smooth. Add the powdered sugar alternately with the milk, then the vanilla, and beat until fluffy.
5. Spread over the cooled cake.

## Chocolate Sheet Cake
> A thin, moist chocolate cake topped with warm fudge icing that sets into a glossy layer.
@ Serves: 20 | Prep: 20 minutes | Cook: 35 minutes
U: Homemade Chocolate Cake Mix
+ For the cake
- 1 bag Homemade Chocolate Cake Mix
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup milk
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 cup hot water or hot coffee
+ For the icing
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/3 cup milk
- 3 1/2 cups powdered sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup chopped pecans (optional)
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9 x 13-inch pan.
2. Beat the cake mix, eggs, milk, oil and vanilla until smooth. Stir in the hot water.
3. Pour into the pan and bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
4. While the cake bakes, melt the butter in a saucepan with the cocoa and milk. Bring just to a boil, remove from the heat and whisk in the powdered sugar and vanilla. Stir in the pecans.
5. Pour the warm icing over the hot cake and spread it gently. Let cool before cutting.

## Cherry Dump Cake
> Just three ingredients and five minutes of work for a bubbling, cobbler-like dessert.
@ Serves: 12 | Prep: 5 minutes | Cook: 50 minutes
U: Homemade Yellow Cake Mix
- 2 cans (21 ounces each) cherry pie filling
- 1 bag Homemade Yellow Cake Mix
- 3/4 cup butter, melted
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and lightly grease a 9 x 13-inch baking dish.
2. Spread the pie filling evenly in the dish.
3. Sprinkle the dry cake mix evenly over the fruit. Do not stir.
4. Drizzle the melted butter over the top, covering as much of the dry mix as possible.
5. Bake for 45 to 50 minutes, until golden and bubbling. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream.

## Fudgy Brownies
> Rich, chewy brownies with the shiny, crackly top everyone fights over.
@ Makes: 16 brownies | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 30 minutes
U: Fudgy Brownie Mix
- 1 bag Fudgy Brownie Mix
- 2/3 cup butter, melted and slightly cooled
- 2 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup chocolate chips or chopped walnuts (optional)
1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Line an 8-inch square pan with parchment paper.
2. Whisk the butter, eggs and vanilla vigorously for 1 minute.
3. Stir in the brownie mix until smooth, then fold in the chocolate chips.
4. Spread in the pan and bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until a toothpick comes out with a few moist crumbs.
5. Cool completely before cutting.

## Chewy Brownie Cookies
> All the fudgy goodness of a brownie in a chewy, crinkly cookie.
@ Makes: about 24 cookies | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 11 minutes
U: Fudgy Brownie Mix
- 1 bag Fudgy Brownie Mix
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1/3 cup vegetable oil
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. Stir the brownie mix, flour, oil and eggs into a thick dough. Fold in the chocolate chips.
3. Drop by rounded tablespoons 2 inches apart.
4. Bake for 9 to 11 minutes, until the edges are set and the tops are crackled. Cool on the pan for 5 minutes.

## Soft Amish Sugar Cookies
> Puffy, tender and buttery, rolled in sugar for a sparkly finish.
@ Makes: about 3 dozen | Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 10 minutes
U: Soft Sugar Cookie Mix
- 1 cup butter, softened
- 1 large egg
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 bag Soft Sugar Cookie Mix
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar, for rolling
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and line baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. Beat the butter, egg and vanilla until creamy. Add the cookie mix and beat on low until a soft dough forms.
3. Roll into 1 1/4-inch balls and roll each in sugar. Place 2 inches apart.
4. Bake for 9 to 11 minutes, just until the bottoms are lightly golden. The tops should still look pale.
5. Cool on the pan for 5 minutes before moving to a rack.

## Yellow Cupcakes
> Fluffy, tender cupcakes topped with creamy vanilla buttercream, ready for any celebration.
@ Makes: 24 cupcakes | Prep: 20 minutes | Cook: 20 minutes
U: Homemade Yellow Cake Mix
+ For the cupcakes
- 1 bag Homemade Yellow Cake Mix
- 1/2 cup butter, softened
- 1 cup milk
- 3 large eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
+ For the buttercream
- 1 cup butter, softened
- 4 cups powdered sugar
- 3 to 4 tablespoons milk
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and line two 12-cup muffin pans with paper liners.
2. Beat the cake mix, butter, milk, eggs and vanilla for 2 minutes.
3. Fill the liners two-thirds full. Bake for 18 to 20 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean. Cool completely.
4. For the buttercream, beat the butter until fluffy, then gradually beat in the powdered sugar, milk and vanilla.
5. Pipe or spread onto the cooled cupcakes.

## Amish Whoopie Pies
> Two soft chocolate cakes sandwiched around a fluffy marshmallow filling. A true Amish country classic.
@ Makes: about 18 whoopie pies | Prep: 30 minutes | Cook: 10 minutes
U: Homemade Chocolate Cake Mix
+ For the cakes
- 1 bag Homemade Chocolate Cake Mix
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
+ For the filling
- 1/2 cup butter, softened
- 1/2 cup vegetable shortening
- 2 cups powdered sugar
- 1 jar (7 ounces) marshmallow creme
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
1. Heat the oven to 375°F and line baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. Stir the cake mix, flour, eggs, oil, milk and vanilla into a thick batter.
3. Drop by 2-tablespoon mounds 3 inches apart.
4. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, until the tops spring back when lightly touched. Cool completely.
5. For the filling, beat the butter and shortening until smooth. Beat in the powdered sugar, then the marshmallow creme and vanilla, until light and fluffy.
6. Spread a generous spoonful of filling on the flat side of half the cakes and top with the remaining cakes.
T: Wrap each whoopie pie in plastic wrap. They keep for 3 days at room temperature, or freeze for up to 2 months.
