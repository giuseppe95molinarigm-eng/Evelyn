Stock your pantry the old-fashioned Amish way, and turn one jar of homemade mix into breakfast, supper and dessert.

Store-bought mixes cost more than they should, and their labels are full of ingredients you can’t pronounce. Amish families have always done it differently. They blend their own flour, leavening, seasonings and spices in big batches, keep them on the pantry shelf, and scoop out exactly what they need on busy days.

The Complete Amish Dry Mix Pantry Cookbook brings that simple, frugal kitchen wisdom into your home. It contains 150 recipes, each with a photo: 45 homemade dry mixes, 90 from-scratch meals and treats you can make with them, and 15 gifts in a jar.

Inside, you’ll find:

  • 45 shelf-stable pantry mixes: all-purpose baking mix, pancake and waffle mixes, cornbread, cake and brownie mixes, bread and pizza dough mixes, cream soup base, gravy mixes, taco seasoning, ranch dressing mix, hot cocoa and more
  • 90 easy recipes made with your mixes: fluffy biscuits, chicken and dumplings, Amish baked oatmeal, whoopie pies, cinnamon rolls, chicken corn soup, Salisbury steak, Amish haystacks, fruit crisps and other family favorites
  • One mix, many meals: each mix is followed by the recipes it makes, so you always know what to cook with what’s already on your shelf
  • 15 gifts in a jar: layered cookies, soups, breads and drinks with ready-to-copy gift tag instructions for holidays, teachers and neighbors
  • Clear, beginner-friendly instructions: exact measurements, yields, storage containers and shelf life for every mix
  • Practical extras: a master shelf-life chart, a mix-to-meal index, a 4-week pantry meal plan, a pantry shopping list and a kitchen conversion chart

Why home cooks love dry mixes:

  • Save money: homemade mixes cost a fraction of boxed versions
  • Know what’s in your food: no preservatives, fillers or hidden additives
  • Save time: do the measuring once and enjoy from-scratch meals all month
  • Be ready for anything: a well-stocked pantry means fewer trips to the store

Whether you’re new to homemade mixes, building a homestead pantry or looking for thoughtful handmade gifts, this book gives you everything you need to fill your shelves with jars that turn into real home cooking.
