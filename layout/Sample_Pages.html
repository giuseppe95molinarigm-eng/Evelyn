All-Purpose Amish Baking Mix

This is the mix you will reach for most often. With the fat already cut into the flour, a batch of biscuits is only a stir and a few minutes in the oven away.

Makesabout 10 cups
Prep15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 8 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup baking powder
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • 1 1/3 cups vegetable shortening

How to Use

For 9 drop biscuits: stir 2 1/4 cups mix with 2/3 cup milk until a soft dough forms. Drop onto a baking sheet and bake at 450°F for 8 to 10 minutes.

To measure, spoon the mix lightly into the cup and level it off. Never scoop straight from the jar, or the biscuits will be dry.

Directions

  1. In a very large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt until evenly combined.
  2. Add the shortening in spoonfuls. Cut it in with a pastry blender, two forks or your fingertips until the mixture looks like coarse crumbs with no pieces larger than a small pea.
  3. Spoon the mix into clean, dry airtight jars or containers without packing it down. Label with the name and date.
KeepsPantry up to 6 weeks; refrigerator up to 3 months; freezer up to 6 months

Pantry Tip

Shortening keeps longer on the shelf than butter. If you prefer an all-butter mix, store it in the refrigerator and use it within 1 month.

Strawberry Shortcake Biscuits

Sweet, tender shortcakes with juicy sugared strawberries and a cloud of whipped cream: the taste of early summer.

Serves6
Prep20 minutes
Cook12 minutes

Ingredients

For the strawberries
  • 1 quart fresh strawberries, hulled and sliced
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
For the shortcakes
  • 2 1/3 cups All-Purpose Amish Baking Mix
  • 3 tablespoons granulated sugar, plus more for sprinkling
  • 3 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1/2 cup milk
To serve
  • Sweetened whipped cream

Directions

  1. Toss the strawberries with 1/4 cup sugar and let stand for at least 20 minutes, until juicy.
  2. Heat the oven to 425°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  3. Stir the baking mix, 3 tablespoons sugar, melted butter and milk until a soft dough forms.
  4. Drop into 6 mounds on the baking sheet and sprinkle with a little sugar.
  5. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes, until golden. Cool slightly.
  6. Split each shortcake, fill with strawberries and juice, and top with whipped cream.
