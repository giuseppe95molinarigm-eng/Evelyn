All-Purpose Amish Baking Mix

This is the mix you will reach for most often. With the fat already cut into the flour, a batch of biscuits is only a stir and a few minutes in the oven away.

Makesabout 10 cups
Prep15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 8 cupsall-purpose flour
  • ¼ cupbaking powder
  • 2 tbspgranulated sugar
  • 1 tbspsalt
  • 1⅓ cupsvegetable shortening

How to Use

For 9 drop biscuits: stir 2¼ cups mix with ⅔ cup milk until a soft dough forms. Drop onto a baking sheet and bake at 450 °F for 8–10 minutes.

To measure, spoon the mix lightly into the cup and level it off. Never scoop straight from the jar, or the biscuits will be dry.

Method

  1. 1
    In a very large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt until evenly combined.
  2. 2
    Add the shortening in spoonfuls. Cut it in with a pastry blender, two forks or your fingertips until the mixture looks like coarse crumbs with no pieces larger than a small pea.
  3. 3
    Spoon the mix into clean, dry airtight jars or containers without packing it down. Label with the name and date.
KeepsPantry up to 6 weeks; refrigerator up to 3 months; freezer up to 6 months

Pantry note

Shortening keeps longer on the shelf than butter. If you prefer an all-butter mix, store it in the refrigerator and use it within 1 month.

Strawberry Shortcake Biscuits

Sweet, tender shortcakes with juicy sugared strawberries and a cloud of whipped cream: the taste of early summer.

Serves6
Prep20 minutes
Cook12 minutes

Ingredients

For the strawberries
  • 1 quartfresh strawberries, hulled and sliced
  • ¼ cupgranulated sugar
For the shortcakes
  • 2⅓ cupsAll-Purpose Amish Baking Mix
  • 3 tbspgranulated sugar, plus more for sprinkling
  • 3 tbspmelted butter
  • ½ cupmilk
To serve
  • Sweetened whipped cream

Directions

  1. 1
    Toss the strawberries with ¼ cup sugar and let stand for at least 20 minutes, until juicy.
  2. 2
    Heat the oven to 425 °F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  3. 3
    Stir the baking mix, 3 tablespoons sugar, melted butter and milk until a soft dough forms.
  4. 4
    Drop into 6 mounds on the baking sheet and sprinkle with a little sugar.
  5. 5
    Bake for 10–12 minutes, until golden. Cool slightly.
  6. 6
    Split each shortcake, fill with strawberries and juice, and top with whipped cream.
